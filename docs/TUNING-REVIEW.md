# The tuning controls, reviewed per card (change 16, 2026-09-21)

Copyright (C) 2026 Botond Sandor. Licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

Every configuration on the shelf, every knob it declares, and the control change 16 gives it - generated from the tree by `node --import ./scripts/gate/ts-ext-register.mjs scripts/knob-census.mjs` (the table's columns are the census's) with the findings written by hand beside each row. The widget rule is `src/lib/tune/view.ts`'s `widgetFor`, total over kind and rungs: a colour knob is the swatch chip and its picker; a knob every one of whose rungs has a word is a segmented row up to four words that fit one line (`SEGMENTED_MAX` 4, `SEGMENT_CHARS_MAX` 40) and a select above; a note ladder past `NOTE_SELECT_MAX` (24) is typed; a ladder of integers is a row of two words at two rungs (12-05) and a stepper above; anything nothing can name is a select of positions. The section is `src/lib/tune/sections.ts`'s `sectionOf`: Look (the colours), Feel, Sound (notes, scales, keys), MIDI (`surprise.ts`'s wire predicate, so the section is what Randomize preserves), Sync (the clock and its divisions) - in that fixed order. The unit is the label's trailing parenthetical (`splitUnit`), shown after the value in the stepper's field; where a label has none, none is shown.

**The one hard rule.** A typed value snaps to the nearest DECLARED rung on Enter or blur (`nearestRung`: ties to the lower value, a number past either end to that end, a word leaves the field on the rung it showed) and never lands between two, because the budget is measured over the declared rungs at the RGB444 corner and the stamp encodes indices. The step boxes and the arrows walk the rungs in VALUE order whatever order the entry declared them in (`valueOrder`), so a descending ladder still steps up in its unit. A MIDI field (CC, channel, note, send) validates as before - a typed value the knob does not offer is refused with the offered values named - and never snaps.

**Fixed in this change** (no Lua moved, no rung value moved - `hash-wire` set, full and Sandbox set equal): seven labels gained their unit word (`Step time (ms)`, `Key delay (ms)`, `Ping speed (ms)`, `Sweep speed (ms)`, `Loop length (points)`, `Interval (min)` for `Minutes`, `Flash width (cells)`); the view's word tables read by the knob's id (`sync` Internal / External, any other boolean under `mode` On / Off, `fill`, `modifier`, `key`, `arms`, `dim`, `spring`, the feel words Soft / Hard, a comma list of integers as its numbers and four RGB triples as their hue words); and a MIDI destination that names no note is a controller number whatever kind the preset declared (`isControllerNumber`) - three findings below are real display bugs the census caught (TRACKPAD's booleans reading External, the presets' Send reading a note name, the HID keys reading note names).

**Recommended, not done** (each is a rung value, order or Lua change, so the stamp or the wire would move): the eight four-rung channel ladders (0 1 9 15) that refuse a typed channel outside them; the unsorted note ladders on POMODORO, QUADRANT, SNAKE and STEPS (a re-sort moves the stamp indices and each default's index); the tick-of-10-ms ladders (CULL's flash, STEPS' and MORPH's and ORBIT's trails, TRACKPAD's fade and COMET's tail) that read as bare numbers - a word table in milliseconds, keyed by id as WHEELS' spring now is, would need no value change if wanted. SNAKE's step time and TRACKPAD's fade are declared descending; the stepper walks them by value, so nothing reads backwards, and a re-sort would move the stamp.

**The counts.** 138 knobs on 27 cards: 39 colour chips, 30 segmented rows, 10 selects, 59 steppers (33 of them MIDI fields). By section: Look 39, Feel 38, Sound 18, MIDI 33, Sync 10. After change 17A (2026-09-23, ARC's LFO output, `docs/MIDI.md`): 140 knobs - 39 colour chips, 31 segmented rows, 11 selects, 59 steppers (33 of them MIDI fields); MIDI 35. The census reads each output knob by its role, as the panel does. After change 17B (2026-09-23, the nineteen hand-authored cards' outputs, `BENCH-2026-09-16.txt` section 17): 253 knobs - 39 colour chips, 81 segmented rows, 26 selects, 107 steppers (81 of them MIDI fields); by section Look 39, Feel 38, Sound 14, MIDI 152, Sync 10. A card's Channel reads 1..16 in the panel (the Values column keeps the wire's 0..15); each output knob's row says which output and role it is.

Legend: **Rungs** is the count; **Values** the literals as declared (the first three and last two of a long ladder); **Default** the shipped rung and its index, with the preview's hold where the browser cannot honour a position (change 8); **Words** the display words where the rungs have them.

## Questions for the user

- CULL's and STAGE's keystroke knobs (the first key, the modifier, the key delay) sit under Sound by the word `key`; say if you want a sixth group for keystrokes, or them under Feel.
- The four-rung channels: say if the eight cards should offer all sixteen channels (a value change, one stamp character still).
- The unsorted note ladders: say if the rungs should be re-sorted (the stamp indices move; a shared link made before the re-sort would land on another note).
- The tick ladders: say if you want them read in milliseconds through a word table (no value change), or left as the firmware's ticks.
