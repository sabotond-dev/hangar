**Splash** (04-UI-SPEC §Screen 1): `position: fixed; inset: 0; z-index: 50`. Bottom to top: black
ground; the glyph field on one `<canvas>` sized to the viewport, drawn **once** on mount and never
again; four solid `#d6ff4e` punched rectangles from the same generator; a CSS halftone grain overlay
`radial-gradient(circle, rgb(214 255 78 / 0.06) 0 0.5px, transparent 0.6px)` at
`background-size: 3px 3px`; and the wordmark `HANGAR` in the Display role (28px / 600 / 0.50em /
uppercase, `#d6ff4e`) on a black plate with 32 px padding. The 0.50em tracking adds a trailing space —
compensate with `margin-inline-end: -0.50em` so the mark stays optically centred.

**Splash timing, full motion:** field in 240 ms `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`; hold 900 ms; dissolve
700 ms `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)`, during which the field and grain go to opacity 0 with
`scale(1.04)`, the coverflow comes up from opacity 0 with its slots going `scale(0.96) → 1`, and the
**wordmark does not fade** — it translates and scales into the header slot over the same 700 ms.
Total 1840 ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: field present immediately, hold 400 ms,
crossfade 200 ms, no scale on anything, the wordmark crossfades into the header slot. Total 600 ms.

**Skip rules:** any `pointerdown`, `keydown` or `wheel` during the field-in or the hold cuts
immediately to the dissolve. The visitor is never *required* to click (D-06 holds); impatience is
respected. No session suppression — reloading plays it again (W-24).

**The coverflow is mounted and already ticking underneath from t = 0** (W-10). That is the brief's
"you arrive and the machines are already running", and it is the reason the splash exists at all: it
covers the simulator's dynamic import.

**Glyph field, deterministic** (04-UI-SPEC §Screen 1, 04-RESEARCH §Architecture Pattern 6): 6–10
rectangular blocks of dense mosaic drawn from the glyph set `x o + □ ◦` at 10 px on a 14 px cell
pitch, per-glyph lime alpha randomised between 0.14 and 0.28, black gaps between blocks, from a
**seeded** mulberry32 PRNG with seed `0x48414E47`, so the field is identical on every load and can be
asserted in a test. Exactly four punched rectangles, each between 40×12 and 220×28 px, aligned to
block boundaries. The glyphs are drawn as canvas **text at 10 px in the browser's default sans** —
04-UI-SPEC exempts canvas-drawn texture from the type scale explicitly, because it is generated pixels
rather than interface type. The three alphas here (0.14–0.28, and the grain's 0.06) sit outside the
token ladder by the same declared exemption.
