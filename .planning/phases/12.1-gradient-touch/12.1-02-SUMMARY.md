Under `@intechstudio/grid-protocol@1.20260825.1135`'s `GridScript.compressScript` after `initLuaFormatter()`,

## Task 3: `G`, `E`, `X` and `Q` in the real VM, every expectation from the table

### The finding: `Q` first, then `G`

The plan's Setup was `G(s,i,e,x,y,0,255,255,255)local m=Q(s,i,e,x,y)`. Run in the real host, a still finger
read DARK after its DOWN: on an onset `G` draws the block and records `B[i]`, then `Q`'s same-id
self-expiry (`library.ts` section 7) reaches `E`, whose `if B[i]then V(B[i])B[i]=nil end` clears the block
`G` just drew; the finger lights only on its first MOVE. A second contact landing on a cell another
contact held has its fresh block cleared by the cross-contact expiry the same way. The research's VM only
ever called `G` alone. **With `Q` first the stale block is what `E` clears, and `G` then draws the fresh
one; every path is right at the same character cost.** The gradient block's header records it for
12.1-03, 12.1-04 and 13-14; the shipped test shape is `local m=Q(s,i,e,x,y)G(s,i,e,x,y,0,255,255,255)if m
then s:gms(0,176,1,m,0)end` (Q's return goes out as CC 1 so the test can read it). Deviation 2.
