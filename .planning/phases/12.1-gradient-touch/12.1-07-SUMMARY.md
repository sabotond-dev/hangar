## The sentence 13-17 reads

`landLua` publishes **four fields** - `systemTimer`, `system`, `setup`, `timer`, in that (write) order -
and the preset landing publishes the same four with the two system strings empty; the store cannot tell
a producer apart, because both reach it as one `ConfigStrings` and the substitution reads the field, not
the route. **The frame count of one RAM leg is four (five with 255/4)**, plus the restore heartbeat; a
surface landing publishes the same four fields.
