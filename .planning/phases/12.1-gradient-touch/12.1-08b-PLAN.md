the map inline at its defaults, let alone at a knob corner. The library route is the only one that fits.

## What forces the checkpoint

D-26 grants this plan a `checkpoint:decision` "only if the vendored edit cannot be confined to the touch
handler". It cannot, for three reasons the tree gives, and the decision is task 01:

1. **The simulator must mirror the compiler or the preview lies.** The eight cards preview through
   `PadSim` (`src/lib/sim/engine.ts`, `createEngine` for `kind: "preset"`), a TypeScript transcription
   of the compiled behaviour, not through the Lua. Since 12.1-05 the pointer already produces the sensor's
   value; if the compiled Lua becomes calibrated and `pad-sim.ts:978-1098` stays on `x*9//d`, a mouse on
   LED 1 shows column 0 on the card and column 1 on the ZONA. That is the loss 11-04's `pad-sim.ts` rows
   were written to prevent. Routing preset previews through wasmoon instead would fetch 271 KB on a cold
   catalog load and break `e2e/catalog.e2e.ts`'s guarantee (the `wasmoon` row in CLAUDE.md).
2. **Both vendored files need one new field on `PadState`** (`touchLibrary?: { kx, ky }`), read by the
   compiler as "emit library calls" and by the simulator as "these are the knots to mirror" - one conduit
   so the two cannot disagree. It is not encoded by `encodeStamp` (`_pad.ts:2670`, a field-wise bit
   writer), so no shape character moves and every captured stamp still decodes (CONT-03).
3. **A preset TRY must land the library** (research 2 above), inverting 12-03's "a preset lands the
   defaults" and 12.1-07's truth "a preset lands the two firmware defaults; the tuner publishes both
   fields empty".

Purpose: D-26 item 2 landed with every vendored byte declared, the preview truthful, every preset
measured, and nothing hardware-verified until the gate. **Scope**: fifteen files and a checkpoint is over
the line for one plan; accepted because the brief names one plan, because tasks 02-04 are one concern
each (the library, the emitted Lua and its two consumers, the wire and the proof), and because splitting
the emitted Lua from its mirror would leave a tree where the preview lies between two commits. If the
orchestrator prefers two plans, the seam is after task 03 (12.1-08c: the wire, the parity test, row 28).
Output: the library at 842 + 873, two vendored files under about twelve manifest rows, eight re-measured
presets, one parity test, row 28. Term `+0 / +3`, audition `+1`.
