## Where this document corrects the research, and where a decision overrules it

`12.1-RESEARCH.md` is measured rather than argued and its firmware findings are the phase's ground
truth. The planner re-ran its harness on Probe C's knots (every 255/0, 255/6, one-slot and re-fit figure
reproduced to the character; the bench case moved from the hypothesis `(116,15)` to the measured
`(120,12)` and still lights cell 16 alone) and found the following, each named here rather than in a
SUMMARY.

### R-1. The knots are measured now; `MEASURED = false` never ships

Research B.5 has `calibration.ts` hold hypothesis values behind a `MEASURED = false` flag until the
probe runs. **Probe C ran before planning** (`BENCH-2026-09-11.txt`, `91ab755`). Plan 01 lands the
measured tables with `MEASURED = true` and a provenance record; the hypothesis values appear nowhere in
the tree. The derivation was checked (D-02): target 10 discarded, `KY[0]` from the corners, separable
within 1 unit.

### R-2. The hold band is a fraction of the pitch, and the research's "same width 12-07 measured" is false on the measured map

Research B.2 reports a seven-value band (69..75) on the hypothesis knots and calls it "the same width
12-07 measured". On the measured knots the band between LED 4 and LED 5 in `x` (pitch 21) is **eight**
values (71..78; up at 79, down at 70), on the outer `x` segment (pitch 6) three (122..124), between LED 2
and 3 (pitch 17) six (35..40) - recomputed by the plan-check, which corrected the planner's first figures. That is the design (D-18): 45/64 of a pitch either side of the LED. Plan 02's smoke test
asserts the band **as a function of the table**, never a literal, and prints the width per segment.

### R-3. The orchestrator's one-slot no-branch does not fit; the honest one is calibration without the gradient

The brief named "a reduced gradient with no expiry clear" as the no-branch. That is research variant H
at 1,043 - over by 135. Measured by the planner: **calibration only** (`U`, `W`, `E`, `Q`, `X`, `A`,
the two tables, `D` dropped) is **882**, 26 free, one slot. D-03 records it as the branch not taken;
the user answered `two-slots`.

### R-4. `docs/CALIBRATION-PROBE.md` does not exist, and neither does `docs/TOUCH-PROBE.md`

The research's Wave 0 names both; `docs/` holds `MIDI-IN-PROBE.md` only. The probe ran from the
research's text. Plan 01 writes `docs/CALIBRATION-PROBE.md` (the 483-character paste re-measured, the
derivation rule, run 1's triples and tables, the re-run procedure) so the calibration bench row has a
document.

### R-5. `host-surface.spec.ts` asserts ten globals and that `glp` is NOT in the library; both invert

The library defines **eighteen** names across the two strings (`H T C P B L KX KY U W E Q X` in 255/0,
`V G N A D` in 255/6), two of them two capitals; `glp`, `glc` and `glim` come back **with their callers
named** (`G` and `V`; `G`; `U` and `G`). `LIBRARY_GLOBALS` is derived from both strings with the regex widened to
`[A-Z]{1,2}`; the "glp is back" assertion is inverted with the reason; the resolved host set becomes
`glag glc glim glp glpfs glt self:gms`. `library.spec.ts` test 2's single-capital rule admits exactly `KX`
and `KY` as the two-capital exceptions. **No firmware Lua global is `KX`, `KY`, `U`, `G`, `V` or `N`** -
grepped over `../grid-fw/common/src/lua/*.lua` by the planner, zero matches; plan 02 repeats the grep
in its own header.

### R-6. A host without `systemTimer` cannot simply "leave `G` undefined" - 255/0 calls `self:tim()`

Research D.2 asks `lua-host.spec.ts` to assert that a missing `systemTimer` leaves `G` undefined. True,
but 255/0 ends with `self:tim()`, so a host given `system: TOUCH_LIBRARY` and no `systemTimer` must
supply **something** for `tim` or the install raises before Setup. Plan 02: when `systemTimer` is
absent the host runs 255/0 with a no-op `tim`, standing for a module whose 255/6 still holds the
firmware default (`print("tick")` defines nothing), and the test asserts `G` undefined **and** that an
entry's first `G(` call raises with `G` named in `errorLog` - the loud failure 13-15 needs.

### R-7. `tune/model.ts` and `wire-pin.spec.ts` move in Band 2, not Band 1

Research F.2 lists both as "free now". `wire-pin.spec.ts` drives the install store's writes and pins the
landed config through it (`:243`, `:374`, `:439`), and `ConfigStrings.systemTimer` is a wire field. The
preview does not need either: `lua-pad-sim.ts:220` passes the library strings to the host directly.
Band 1 exports `TOUCH_LIBRARY_TIMER` from `library.ts` and passes it there; plan 07 threads it through
`ConfigStrings`, the tuner's landing and the pin.

### R-8. 13-07 has landed; `HeroSurface.svelte` is stable and is still not edited in Band 1

The research cites it as "being edited now". It landed at `ed7926c`. D-21 keeps the orchestrator's
rule: Band 1 touches neither canvas; plan 08 adds the `"y"` token to `HeroSurface.svelte:137` after
13-07..13-11 have landed, and to `Coverflow.svelte:520` only if 13-09 has not deleted it.

### R-9. The snapshot key is "the next version", not necessarily `v3`

13-12 (wave 13, before Band 2) writes `snapshot.ts` for per-page keying and may add a key of its own.
D-22: plan 07 reads `13-12-SUMMARY.md` and names the key it adds as the next version after whatever
13-12 left. The rule does not move.

### R-10. `sequence.spec.ts:466` is the assertion that halves

The research names "sequence.spec.ts 'no frame addresses 255/4 or 255/6'". It is the loop at `:466`
inside the write-order test, asserting `element === 255 && (event === 4 || event === 6)` is false for
every write. Plan 06 halves it: 255/4 still never; 255/6 exactly once per install, first.

### R-11. The audition table admits new rows; 12-12's "fixed at 23" was about JOYSTICK, not about growth

12-12 declined a JOYSTICK row because the document covers hand-authored configurations by its title.
12-10 added row 23 and moved `ROW_COUNT`. The three rows this phase appends name hand-authored entries
and `any`, and move `ROW_COUNT` to 26.

### R-12. The gate's first ten minutes are the re-observation, not the probe re-run

The brief asked for both to be first. D-24 orders the re-observation first, the re-run second, with the
reason; the rows are listed below in that order.

### R-14. Layer 0 is the alert layer, and there is no floor

Found by the plan-check, not by the research: `grid_alert_all_set` (`grid_led.c:260-271`) rewrites layer
0's colour on every LED and forces its min to 0 - on a CONFIG write, on page-discard completion, on a
refused page change, on a TX overflow and at boot. D-11 therefore has `G` carry the colour and re-assert
it on its four cells every call (255/6 714, every re-fit cheaper); and `glc(...,1)`'s trailing `1` forces
min to 0 (`l_grid_led_layer_color`, nargs 6), so research C's "byte-6 floor" paragraph is wrong and
audition row 25(f) asks the opposite question.

### R-15. `src/routes/dev/skeleton/+page.svelte` consumes `FetchedSet`

It reads three fields and compares three in its store proof (`:356-393`); in no research row. Plan 06
widens it to four so the fourth string is inside the skeleton's no-op proof.

### R-13. The bench-case coordinates in the research are hypothesis values

`(116, 15)` -> measured `(120, 12)`; `N(116,15)=16` -> `N(120,12)=16`; `U(x)` for `x = 0, 11, 20, 29, 64,
72, 116, 127` -> on the measured knots `0, 53, 92, 128, 256, 280, 435, 512`. Every expectation in the
tests is computed from `calibration.ts`, so none of these numbers is typed into a spec.
