### The three derived functions

- `sensorAt(u, axis, max = 127)` - the FORWARD map: LED coordinate to sensor value, piecewise-linear
  over the knots, floored, saturating at the outer knots outside 0..8, then `* (max + 1) / 128`
  floored for hi-res (`sensorAt(4, "x", 1023) === 512 === 64 * 8`).
- `calibratedAxis(v, axis)` - the TS twin of the Lua `U(v,k)`:
  `v=glim(v,k[1],k[9])for i=1,8 do if v<k[i+1]then return i*64-64+(v-k[i])*64//(k[i+1]-k[i])end end return 512`,
  with `Math.floor` for the `//` and the same clamp. Plan 02's `lua-smoke.spec.ts` drives the Lua `U`
  against it for every `v` in 0..127 on both axes; this plan asserts the twin's own properties.
- `renderKnots()` as above. Also exported: `LED_STEP = 64`, `AXIS_SPAN = 512`, `knotsOf(axis)`.

### The four tests (`calibration.spec.ts`, 4)

4. **the TS twin of `U`** - `K[n] -> n * 64`, `0 -> 0`, `127 -> 512`, integer and inside 0..512 and

## Deviations from the plan

value at a knot midpoint within 1 of `n * 64 + 32`" holds only for an even pitch. `calibratedAxis`
takes the integer the sensor reports; for an odd pitch `d` the midpoint is a half-unit the floor drops,
and the floor division loses up to `32/d` more, so the deviation is exactly `1 + floor(32/d) =
