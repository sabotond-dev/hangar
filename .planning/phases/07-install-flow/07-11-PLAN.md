both are `disabled` while `session.writeLock` is true - on every leg, the store leg explicitly - with
`Not while HANGAR is writing to your ZONA.` beneath the pair.

line: the durable form when the module's serial was answered and the record is in the browser, the
honest session-only form when it was not (D-04 amended) - it names no control, because the panel
may be closed when it is read. And `FORGET THIS ZONA`'s explanation now says the copy stays (Z-13):
revoking a permission is never a reason to destroy somebody's only copy of their own configuration.

`session.announce` (07-04) into the one region it renders, and the `lost` precedence is the session's
own `writeLock` guard. This plan proves that by scan rather than by touching the file.

`keep-on-device` visible-and-disabled with the reason inside `connect-status`; it is extended - not

sentence.
