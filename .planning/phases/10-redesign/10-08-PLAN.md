<objective>
The 4,096-colour lattice, and the sweep restructure that makes it affordable — both before the picker
is built.

Wave 8. Blocked on 10-07. This is the plan with the phase's only irreversible user-visible failure
mode, and the plan whose runtime must be planned rather than discovered in CI.

**What the pad can actually show.** `quantiseColour` snaps every stored channel to a multiple of 17
(`_pad.ts:490-493`), so the state holds exactly **RGB444: sixteen steps per channel, 4,096 colours and
no more**, and it does so specifically so the URL stamp round-trips. One layer emits at most
**254/512 = 49.6%** of what you ask for, and there is **no gamma correction anywhere** in the WS2812
path. A picker offering 16.7 million colours would be a lie in three separate ways at once.
`knobs.preset.ts:135-137` already wrote that argument. **The resolution is not to refuse D-06; it is
to build on the lattice.**

**Collision 1 — the 908 wall, and the `tpad` claim that was false.** `knobs.preset.ts:688` reads
`tpad: [tapKnob, pointerKnob, scrollKnob]`. **The trackpad has no colour knob**, so its measured
907-of-908 worst state cannot be pushed over by a colour literal — there is no colour literal in it.
Six of the nine presets carry a colour knob; three do not. **The dearest state on any preset that has
one is `ninepads` at 640 of 908, with 268 characters free.** The disabled-detent machinery ships as a
guard that today never fires, and the sweep records the margin as a number so the day it stops being
268 the suite says so.

**Collision 2 — the URL stamp, and the one thing that must not be got wrong.** Format `x` encodes one
base-32 character per knob and `stamp-roundtrip.sweep.spec.ts:167-172` asserts a
`STAMP_OPTION_CEILING` of 32 precisely because a 33rd option **would not overflow loudly — it would
truncate silently and land a shared link on the wrong colour.** 4,096 is 128 times the ceiling. So
format `w` is claimed. **Format `x` keeps decoding forever. The old format is never removed, only
stopped being emitted.**

**V-03 lands here, in two tasks rather than one.** §17 asks for the restructure "before the picker is
built" with Pass B costing all 4,096. But Pass B enumerates the colour knob's option list, and today
that list is the card's own colour plus a five-member palette — enumerating 4,096 before the lattice
exists would be enumerating unreachable states. **Task 1 restructures at today's option counts and
records the wall clock. Task 2 lands the lattice and Pass B becomes 4,096 per colour knob.** Both are
upstream of the picker at 10-10.

Purpose: TUNE-01's colour clause; TUNE-05 kept unreachable with its margin measured; SHARE-01 and
SHARE-03 extended without breaking a link.
Output: two sweep passes, one lattice knob, one new format letter, one captured fixture, three
wall-clock figures.

**The dearest literal, and why "dearest" needed correcting.** `glc(a, layer, r, g, b, 1)` writes three
decimal literals; under RGB444 each is one, two or three digits, so the three together are **3 to 9
characters**. Measured on aurora's Setup: `0,0,0` costs **253**, `0,204,255` costs **257**,
`119,187,238` costs **259**, and so does `255,255,255` — *a card that emits its colour once charges one
character per digit, so every three-digit-per-channel literal ties.* `255,255,255` pulls ahead only on
`ninepads`, whose checkerboard emits a dimmed second copy. **Pin Pass A at a genuinely dearest literal
per preset and verify it by measurement**, not by picking `255,255,255` and hoping.

**The Lua colour sample is 27 literals per colour knob** — the 3 × 3 × 3 combinations of a one-, two-
and three-digit channel — and it is **length-complete**. The justification is already written at
`lua-entries.sweep.spec.ts:358-384`: moving one knob changes an event's length by *pure literal
arithmetic*, occurrences times the difference in literal length. Every reachable literal length (3 to
9 digits) and every per-channel digit count in every position appears in the 27, including the
all-longest corner the budget claim rests on. **Quote that passage; do not restate it.**

**The lattice knob's shape.** `options` become the full reachable lattice and `read()` / `apply()`
become **index ↔ RGB444 arithmetic** rather than array lookups: `i` in `[0, 4096)`,
`r = ((i >> 8) & 15) * 17`, `g = ((i >> 4) & 15) * 17`, `b = (i & 15) * 17`, and the inverse.
`read(apply(state, i)) === i` holds by construction. **Rejected and named so it is not reinvented:** a
picker that writes outside the knob system directly into the `PadState` colour field. It forks the
state model and breaks `SURPRISE ME`, `RESET ALL`, the default marker, the fit ladder and the
forecast, all of which are knob-index machinery.

**`STAMP_OPTION_CEILING` stays 32** and stays asserted. The lattice knob is exempt **by format**, not
by raising the ceiling: a compiler entry's colour field already encodes RGB444 directly in formats
`a`/`b`/`c`, and a Lua entry's colour rides format `w`'s 12 raw bits. **Raising the ceiling would
silently truncate every other knob's guard.**
