<objective>
CLEAR, part two: the controls, the confirmation, and the sequence D-04 asked for — without flattening
SAFE-02.

Wave 13. Blocked on 10-12, which built the machine this plan renders.

**D-04's tension is exact.** SAFE-02's whole content is that `TRY ON DEVICE` and `KEEP ON DEVICE` are
never equal-weight, and today they are three tiers apart with a hairline, a tuning region and another
control between them. **"One natural sequence" must not become "three equal buttons in a row".**

> **A sequence is made of a caption, an order and an enablement. It is not made of equal weights.**

| Channel | How it carries the sequence |
|---|---|
| **A caption** | region 6 gains one: **`NEXT`** (4), Micro, `--color-ink-quiet`, directly under the existing hairline. One word, inside the two-word uppercase rule. Before a try-on it still reads right: it is what comes next |
| **DOM and visual order** | unchanged from Z-03: `PUT BACK` → `KEEP ON DEVICE` → **`CLEAR`** → `COPY LINK`, at a 16px rhythm, one layout at every width |
| **Enablement choreography** | the strongest channel, and **it already exists**. Before a try-on, everything under `NEXT` is disabled with its reason in its own cell. The instant `settled` lands, `PUT BACK` and `KEEP ON DEVICE` go live **together**. That transition *is* the sequence, and it is behaviour rather than decoration |
| **The honesty slot above** | already says what the primary does before the click. **Nothing is added** |

**Explicitly not done:** no step numerals, no connecting rule or bracket, no progress indicator, no
shared background, no equalised widths, **no accent on any sequence marker**.

**A-24 — CLEAR's tier is Bare, and tracking is the distinguishing channel.** Every other label on the
site is Micro at 0.18em; CLEAR is at **0.28em** — the one channel that says *this one is not like the
others* without a colour, without a box, and without an icon this phase promised not to ship. It is
also the ZONA landing's own idiom, where the whole page's character comes from tracking, **and it is
grep-checkable**: `letter-spacing: 0.28em` appears on exactly one control.

**A-25 — no red, and the reversal is one line.** With every colour deleted, CLEAR is distinguishable
from `KEEP ON DEVICE` on five channels. **Red would say *dangerous* where the truth is *deliberate***,
and `--color-over` reused from a meter where it means "over 908" would make both meanings weaker. The
reversal (Open item 6) is: `--color-over` as the confirmation block's 1px border only, never a fill,
never a label, never the affirmative, plus one line in `src/app.css`'s header comment going from three
uses to four. `identity.spec.ts` needs no change; the token already exists. **State that cost.**

Purpose: SAFE-02 kept verbatim; SAFE-05 extended; DEGR-02 extended; row H handed over.
Output: two components, one caption, one hairline, one `CLEARED` block, two e2e titles, one runbook
