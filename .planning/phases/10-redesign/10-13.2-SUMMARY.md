# Phase 10 Plan 13.2: The Aesthetic Pass, Corrected Summary

Two defects against the brief's own words — the CRT feel *"without being overwhelming and destructive
to the UX"* — fixed by moving where an existing field paints and how an existing layer draws an unlit
cell, and both proved in a browser because a source scan is structurally blind to paint order.

---

## FIRST AND PLAINLY: the two places a shipped document said something the built site contradicts

### 1. §19.1a specified the marks and never specified where they may land

The section fixes **pitch, arm, stroke, colour, composition, element and where it may not go** — and
says nothing at all about position within the surface it is on. Both roots are **ancestors of the
reading column** rather than neighbours of it, so the field painted straight across the prose.

Measured on the built site at 1280×900, before anything was changed:

| Surface | Finding |
|---|---|
| `/browse/`, populated | **418** text-bearing elements under a lattice root with **no opaque ancestor** between them and the field |
| `/browse/`, empty result | **44** |
| Chosen panel on `/c/arc/` | **55** |
| `HANGAR` wordmark box | **2** lattice crossings inside it |
| Browse headline box | **13** |

And by eye: crosses on `Available after a try-on.`, on `Needs a copy of what is on your ZONA first.`,
on `CLEAR`, on `MIDI channel`, on every knob readout and on every catalog-card description.

**The ruling:** the lattice is a **ground**, and a ground is visible exactly where nothing is standing
on it. §19.1a is **rewritten** with a *Where it may paint* row and five paragraphs of reason — A-55,
A-56, A-57 — rather than deleted, and its geometry table is untouched because it was never wrong.

### 2. `PadFrame.svelte`'s header carried half a diagnosis

It said, and had said since Phase 4: *"without the dots an unlit pad is a black square"*. **The dots
were there and Trackpad was still a black square.** The reason is one layer further up: the cell
**structure** on a pad face is drawn by **Layer 3, the gutter grid**, and Layer 3 is painted in
`--color-ground`. A black grid divides nothing when the cells behind it are also black. Every other
card gets its structure for free from its **lit** cells; the one configuration that lights none of
them therefore got none, and 81 lamps collapsed into one rectangle.

The header now says that, and A-58 records it.

## Defect 1 — the fix chosen, and the one rejected

The brief offered **(a)** knock the field back with one declaration, and **(b)** stop it painting
under the surfaces that carry prose. **(b) was chosen.**

**(a) was rejected on principle, not on cost.** It helps — a fainter cross is a fainter cross — but it
**changes the symptom's amplitude and not its cause**. A cross would still sit on the `H` of `HANGAR`
and on the `y` of *Everything on the shelf*. The brief's own sentence, and the reference's own device,
is that the marks belong **in the surround, not across the subject**, and that is a statement about
**position**. An opacity is not a position.

**(b) cost four declarations across four files and no new node anywhere.**

```css
.lattice > :where(*) {
  background-color: var(--color-ground);
}
```

**`:where()` is load-bearing.** It zeroes the compound, so the selector weighs 0,1,0 — the same as a
bare class:

1. A Svelte-scoped rule compiles to `.foo.svelte-<hash>` at 0,2,0 and therefore **wins**. Any
   component with an opinion about its own background keeps it, automatically, without appearing on
   any list. The rule is a **default**, not an override.
2. It adds **no node** — no wrapper, no pseudo-element, no class attribute on any component. That was
   the brief's stated condition for preferring (b).

**And it is declared ABOVE `.pill`, which is warning 6 of this phase arriving on schedule.** `.pill`'s
`background: transparent` is also 0,1,0, so specificity cannot separate them and **source order
decides**. Written where it belongs — at the bottom of the file beside the lattice's own comment — it
would win the tie and put an opaque box behind a control that had declared a transparent one. It was
written there first, and moved. `instrument.spec.ts` scan 6 asserts the order as well as the
`:where(`.

### The three declared exceptions, and how the third was found

| File | Selector | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `src/routes/browse/+page.svelte` | `.grid` | `transparent` | The card wall is where the field earns its keep — it paints in the **gutters between** the cards. Grounding it would delete the lattice from most of the page |
| `src/lib/ui/CatalogCard.svelte` | `.card` | `var(--color-ground)` | A card is four levels below a root, so the ground rule cannot reach its description. Without this, the grid's exception means "over thirty-six descriptions" instead of "between thirty-six cards" |
| `src/lib/ui/BrowseGrid.svelte` | `.empty` | `var(--color-ground)` | **Found by the DOM walk with `?q=zzzzqqq`, not by reading a template.** It renders *instead of* the card wall inside that same transparent grid — the one screen where the page has nothing to show would have been the one screen where the field was worst |

**A-56 asserts the count as well as the members**, so a fourth has to be argued for in the spec before
it can ship.

### What it costs Layer G, computed rather than estimated (A-57)

An opaque background occludes everything behind it, and Layer G is `body::before` at `z-index: -1`.
Its halftone no longer shows through where these blocks paint.

**Layer G's declaration, its route scope and `aesthetic.e2e.ts` test 3 are unchanged** — all three
were re-run and are green. What changes is how much bare ground the page has. The arithmetic: the dot
is `rgb(214 255 78 / 0.04)` in a 3px cell with a solid core out to 40% of it, so its area-averaged
lift over black is `0.04 × π × 0.4² ≈ 0.008` of the token — **about one part in 255 on the green
channel**, dithered at 3px, which reads as grain rather than as a cast. The vignette half is
`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55)` over `--color-ground`: black over black, and free.

## Defect 2 — the fix, and the four things it does not do

```css
.dots {
  background-color: color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-line-soft) 25%, transparent);
  /* the dot field, unchanged */
}
```

It sits **behind the canvas**, so a lit cell covers it and only cells the configuration left at alpha
0 show it. Layer 3's black gutters then cut it into 81 dark lamps.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does it invent lit cells? | **No.** It is the absence of a light drawn so it can be seen, behind the canvas, and the canvas is unchanged |
| Does it animate? | **No.** Scan 8 asserts `.dots` declares no `animation`, `transition`, `transform` or `filter` |
| Does it change what the config does? | **No.** No file under `src/vendor/`, `src/lib/catalog/` or `src/lib/sim/` was touched |
| Is it a new colour? | **No.** `color-mix` of the existing `--color-line-soft`. Nine tokens, three hexes, `identity.spec.ts` still **7** |
| Is it stronger than other cards' unlit cells? | **No** — 0.05 is a quarter of the dot's own 0.2, strictly dimmer at every pixel, so the dot stays the brightest mark in every unlit cell. Scan 8 caps the mix at 50% |
| Global or scoped to `tpad`? | **Global**, and that is the ruling's own constraint. *"The cell structure at the same strength the other cards' unlit cells have"* — scoping it to Trackpad would make the one card that can never light the **brightest** unlit face on the site |

`front-door.ts` and `browse.e2e.ts` is **0 lines**. All green. `scripts/gen-og.mjs` renders from the

## The finding that cost a red run

**`color-mix(in srgb, ...)` does not serialise back to `rgba()`.** Both engines return
`color(srgb 0.839216 1 0.305882 / 0.05)` — chromium and webkit at a phone viewport, measured. The
first version of test 5 parsed only `^rgba?\(...\)` and fell through to its default of 1, so it
reported the 0.05 wash as **fully opaque** and the suite came back `101 passed, 2 failed`.

It matters in both directions: in the occlusion walk the same reader would have called a genuinely
opaque `color()` background **transparent**, and over-reported. Both readers now handle the slashed
form; they are the same rule written twice because the walk is serialised into the page and can close
over nothing, and **an assertion holds them together** — the walk returns the one colour both can see,
and the test checks they read the same alpha out of it.

## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 3 — Blocking] `git checkout --` destroyed uncommitted work during the negative checks

**Found during:** Task 1, running negative checks 1–3.
**Issue:** The restore step was written as `git checkout -- <path>`, which restores to **HEAD** — and
at that point `src/app.css` and `src/routes/browse/+page.svelte` carried the whole task's uncommitted
work. Both files were reverted to the committed version, silently, and the `sha256` after the run
differed from the one before it.
**Fix:** Both files re-applied from the exact text, then the negative checks re-run with a
**scratch-copy restore** (`cp` from a `keep/` directory taken before the perturbation). Every
subsequent perturbation in both tasks used that idiom, and every restoration is `sha256`-identical.
**Recorded rather than smoothed over**, because the sha comparison the plan already required is what
caught it: `9cca308f…` before, `8fe719fb…` after. Without that line it would have been invisible until
a test failed for a reason nobody could explain.

### 2. [Rule 2 — Missing critical] A fourth surface, found by walking rather than by reading

`BrowseGrid.svelte`'s `.empty` was not in the plan's exception table when the plan was written. The
DOM walk with a query matching no entry found it. The plan's own instruction was to *"add it to the
table and say so — do not quietly widen the ground rule"*, and that is what happened: three exceptions
where the plan projected two-plus-one, named in A-56 with the method that found it.

### 3. [Rule 1 — Bug] The alpha reader was wrong for the colour the plan itself introduced

See "The finding that cost a red run". Caught by a real suite run — `101 passed, 2 failed` — rather
than by review.

### 4. Scope, declared: `10-CONTEXT.md` was edited

The brief named four documents to amend. `10-CONTEXT.md` is a fifth, and it was edited to add **D-22**
because every A-number in §19 cites a D, and §19.3 would otherwise have cited a decision with no
register entry.

### 5. The brief's assumption about `instrument.spec.ts:850` was wrong, and the plan says so

`:849-851` reads `expect(declared.size).toBeGreaterThan(6)` — a **non-vacuity floor** that interpolates
the observed count into its message. There is no equality to "re-derive". Recorded as **A-60**, and
the whole lattice fix lives outside `.lattice::before` anyway, so the rule gained no declaration.

### 6. One thing the brief asked for that could not be measured as asked

The brief asked for *"the crossings-inside-a-glyph-box count before and after"*. **That number does
not move and cannot**: the field's geometry is byte-identical, so two crossings still fall inside the
wordmark's box. The measurement was replaced by two that mean what the brief meant — the occlusion
walk (418 → 0) and the lit-pixel count inside four prose boxes — and the substitution is stated above
rather than buried.
