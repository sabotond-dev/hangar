## FIRST AND PLAINLY: the three places D-15, D-16 and D-17 disagree with something already shipped

### 1. D-16's register line is false as a statement about the rendered tree

**The ruling:** it is implemented as a line of **authorship**, and the SUMMARY, the spec gate and the
new file's header all say so.

§19.1g states the line as *"the front-door register is everything inside `.front-door`; the instrument
register is everything that is not"*, and the plan restates it verbatim in `<interfaces>`. **Against
the shipped tree that is false, and provably so.** `src/routes/+page.svelte` **and**
`src/routes/c/[id]/+page.svelte` both mount `FrontDoor.svelte`, whose root element carries
`class="front-door"`. `FrontDoor.svelte:309` mounts `Coverflow.svelte`, which mounts
`ChosenPanel.svelte`, `TryOnDevice.svelte`, `CopyLink.svelte` and `TuningRegion.svelte`. **So the
panels, the tuning region and the whole device flow — every surface D-16 moves INTO the instrument
register — are DOM descendants of `.front-door`, and have been since wave 4.**

The plan's own two lattice roots prove it: one of them, `ChosenPanel.svelte`, is a descendant of
`.front-door` on both routes that render it. A DOM-containment reading would forbid the plan's own
task 2.

**Why the authorship reading works and is not a dodge.** Svelte scopes a component's styles to that
component, so `TryOnDevice.svelte`'s pill is authored outside the front door's stylesheet even though
its button paints inside the front door's box. Scan 1 therefore asserts: every moving or texturing CRT
layer is **scoped** to `.front-door`, and no instrument rule is **authored** inside `FrontDoor.svelte`
or under a `.front-door` selector, anywhere in `src/`. That is checkable, it lives in one place, and it
cannot be moved in one file and not the other.

### 2. D-16 is wrong that "the ground is already the hero shell's"

**The ruling:** Layer G stays on every route and is **reclassified**, and the wrongness is asserted by
name inside the failure message rather than recorded only here.

D-16 reads *"the ground and the roll bar are already the hero shell's"*. **The roll bar is** — Layers R
and T live in `.crt-band` inside `FrontDoor.svelte`, and `/browse/` has no `.crt-band` at all. **The
ground is not** — Layer G is `body::before` in `src/app.css`, on every route, and 10-04 measured
`/browse/` explicitly as *"Layer G alone"*. Scan 1 asserts Layer G's selector does **not** name
`.front-door`, with the reason in the message: a halftone whose density describes a page is D-15
reference B's own image-making device rather than a screen effect, and it is the only one of the four
layers that neither moves nor scans.

### 3. D-16's "load-bearing edit" to Layer S was already made, four waves earlier

**The ruling:** Layer S's selector is **unchanged**, and A-38's second justification is what this plan
added rather than a selector.

`PadFrame.svelte:170` already reads `:global(.front-door) .pad::after`, and has since wave 4 — applied
then as §8.5's declared performance fallback after webkit-phone measured **61 ms p95 against a 2 ms
threshold** (chromium **0.00 ms**). D-16's load-bearing edit is therefore a **no-op on the selector and
a real change to the reason**: one line now has two independent justifications, so a faster engine
cannot argue it back. `e2e/aesthetic.e2e.ts` test 3, which already asserted the browse grid carries
Layer G and nothing else, now holds two rules with one assertion and was **re-run unchanged**.

## The silent no-op the source scans could not see

**The lattice shipped once painting nothing at all, and every scan over it was green.** With
`position: relative` alone, two screenshots of `/browse/` — one as authored, one with
`.lattice::before { display: none }` injected — came back **byte-identical at 8,492 bytes**.

The cause is CSS 2.1 Appendix E's painting order. Without a stacking context on `.lattice`, a
negative-`z-index` pseudo-element belongs to the nearest ancestor stacking context — the root — and
paints at step 2, while `body`'s own `background: var(--color-ground)` is an in-flow block background
painting at step 3. Opaque black, straight over the top.

`isolation: isolate` is the fix. It is now **asserted by name** in scan 3 with the measurement in its
own message, because its absence is invisible to every other assertion in the file. Both roots
re-proved after the fix, against a build made with wrangler stopped:

| Surface | With lattice | With `::before` hidden | Paints |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/browse/` root | 10,608 bytes | 8,492 bytes | yes |
| `ChosenPanel` root | 56,296 bytes | 51,358 bytes | yes |

`isolation: isolate` **is** a grouping property — it forces `transform-style: flat` on descendants — so
it is legal here for exactly the reason the mask is, and scan 3 proves that position by **reading**
`Coverflow.svelte`: `.panel` sits at div-depth **0** in that file, a top-level sibling of `.band`,
outside the 3D context `.stage` establishes. `Coverflow.svelte` is byte-untouched.

## The `+`-separated metadata block: the SEVENTH `--font-mono` use, argued out loud

**A-44 wins over the plan's body and over §19.1c, and the disagreement is reported here.** The plan's
task 3 says *"It is `--font-mono`'s **sixth** named use"* three times, and §19.1c and §19.1f say the
same. **A-44 supersedes all of them**: 10-10 spent the sixth on the picker's RGB triple, so §19.1c's
count double-counted it. The observed number in the tree is **seven declarations across six files**
(`Knob.svelte` carries two), and scan 5 asserts the **list**, not the count alone — a seventh use
somewhere else would keep the number right and still be a defect.

`ColourPicker.svelte:599-604` demanded that a seventh have its argument made out loud rather than
inherited. **The argument, as written into `CatalogCard.svelte` beside the declaration:**

> The fifth and the sixth qualify on one half of W-03's rule — *a number that changes as a pointer moves
> and must not jitter horizontally*. **That argument does not describe this block**, which is static from
> the moment the card mounts and never changes at all. It qualifies on **the other half of the same
> rule: machine text whose columns must hold** — and the claim is about the **stack** rather than about
> any one card. Thirty-six blocks of three fields sit in a grid, separated by a character that is only
> legible *as* a separator when it lands in the same place on every card. In a proportional face two ids
> of different widths put their plus signs at unrelated offsets and the wall reads as three cards' worth
> of noise; in a fixed advance the fields are columns and the wall reads as a table. That is reference
> A's device exactly, and **no single-card argument would have reached it**.

`tabular-nums` is declared with **no digit in the block today**, and that is stated in the source as
deliberate rather than left as cargo: the fields are catalog tokens now, and the day one carries a
number the columns must not move on the day it arrives rather than on the day somebody notices.

The count in the picker's own comment chain was updated, so the next reader inherits **seven**.

**§13.0's character enumeration did not need an edit, and that is stated rather than left silent.** The
plan asks for U+002B to join the copy specs' enumerations. It does not belong there: the `+` is markup
furniture in `CatalogCard.svelte`, and **no string in any copy module carries it**, so
`src/lib/catalog/copy.spec.ts` and `src/lib/tune/copy.spec.ts` enumerate exactly what they enumerated
before — a forbidden set plus one scoped permission for U+2212 in `forecastDelta`. Before and after are
identical. Scan 5 holds the separator instead, at the element that renders it.
