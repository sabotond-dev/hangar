<objective>
Browse at sixteen chips and two sorts, and a front door that is the entry to it rather than its rival.

Wave 7. Blocked on 10-06, which closed the vocabulary this plan renders.

Four pieces, and three of them are removals:

**D-11 — Newest goes.** `addedAt` has three distinct values across thirty-six entries and one of them
covers twenty, so ordering by date says nothing. `sort.spec.ts:125` **self-adjusts** — it already
reads `BROWSE_SORTS.length * LISTING.length * (LISTING.length - 1)` and follows the constant. What
needs editing is `:82`, whose literal `["featured", "newest", "name"]` and the words "the three
sorts" are hand-written, and the `NEWEST` date-block test at `:179`, which 09-02 restructured and
which is **retired by name**. That asymmetry is worth a negative check of its own.

**And the e2e delta is 0. Measured, not hedged.** `e2e/browse.e2e.ts:268` is a single title —
`test("sorting reorders the grid and the address")` — whose body walks FEATURED, then NAME, then
NEWEST at `:289-311`, then FEATURED again. Removing the Newest sort deletes assertions *inside* that
body; **no title disappears**. `browse-webkit.e2e.ts` never names `newest`. So this plan's e2e delta
is **0**, and the phase total is **`BASE_E2E + 12`**, not `+10`.

**A-19 — the toolbar's tag row becomes two facet rows.** `FOR` first, ten chips; `FEELS` second, six.
The count-derived row and the outsider chip retire together, because the vocabulary is closed and no
active facet member can fall outside the standing rows. `disabledTags()` stays and becomes rare.

**A-20 — no shared link is dropped on the floor.** `?tag=` is accepted for one release. A **mapped**
value becomes its facet chip. An **unmapped** value becomes `?q=`. Dropping it silently would break
exactly the links most likely to exist, *because a singleton tag is what somebody sends when they mean
this one card*.

**A-22 — the ring stays at eight and gains a `FOR` link row**, answering Open item 3. The ten workflow
terms render on `/` as **links**, not checkboxes: a link keeps the front door prerendered, keeps its
`<head>` intact, and keeps the page import-free. The reversal — growing the ring — is blocked by
`front-door.spec.ts:110-112`'s `preview === "padsim"` requirement, which twenty-seven of the
thirty-six entries fail; record that as a deferred item rather than pre-empting it.

Purpose: CAT-02 amended to two sorts; CAT-03 and CAT-04 rendered.
Output: one sort removed, two facet rows, one URL migration, one link row, one AND→OR e2e rewrite,
and **no e2e title lost**.
