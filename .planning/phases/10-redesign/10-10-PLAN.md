<objective>
The stylized RGB picker, built on the hardware's own 4,096-colour lattice.

Wave 10. Blocked on 10-09, and through it on 10-08's lattice knob and restructured sweep — both of
which exist so this plan is a rendering problem rather than a runtime problem.

**A-09 — the fence, and the whole of it:**

> The pad exemption extends from colour swatches to **the picker's detents and its result pad, and to
> nothing else in this phase.** Every filled pixel inside the picker is a colour the ZONA will emit at
> that index, drawn as a **flat fill of an exact stored RGB444 value**. Every line, ring, label, tick,
> number and caption around it is lime on black.
>
> **There is no third category.** An HSV gradient field, a hue ring, a saturation/value square, a
> continuous slider, a CSS gradient of any kind on a rail, and `<input type="color">` are each
> **forbidden by name**, for the same reason: every one of them authors colour in CSS, and every one
> of them implies a resolution the state does not have.

**A-10 — one picker per panel, not one per knob.** Three rails per colour knob would put nine rails
and three extra canvases on `console`, `strip` and `forge`, breaking the six-canvas budget and the
amended TUNE-01 in one stroke. The rails edit whichever colour knob a compact word-row selector names.
**14 entries show no selector, 14 show two options, 3 show three** — verified by the planner against
the shipped tree on 2026-09-08 and reconciled again here.

**The result is a pad, not a swatch, and that is the argument.** One layer emits at most 49.6% of what
you ask for and there is no gamma correction anywhere in the WS2812 path, so **a flat swatch is the
lie and the miniature pad is the truth**: it answers "what will this look like on my ZONA" instead of
"what does this hex look like on my monitor".

**Nothing new is invented.** Three rails are the shipped detent-track widget (05-UI-SPEC's `n ≥ 9`
skin) instantiated three times; the knob selector is Phase 5's word-row widget, its third use after
`SORT` and `SCREEN`. **No new widget, no new keyboard model, no new focus behaviour.**

Purpose: D-06 answered; TUNE-01 amended by one clause; TUNE-05's guard built and measured.
Output: one component, one gate file, one widget-selection amendment.
</objective>

<interfaces>
**The parts, and each one's contract.**

| Part | Contract |
|---|---|
| **One picker per panel** | a panel declares one `ColourPicker`: three rails, one result pad, and — **only when the entry has more than one colour knob** — one compact selector naming which knob the rails are editing |
| **The knob selector** | Phase 5's word-row widget, unchanged in markup and behaviour: a `role="radiogroup"` of real `<input type="radio">` inside `<label>`s, **one tab stop**, arrow keys move and select, selected option in `--color-accent` (**reserved-list item 8 — a selector of knobs is knob vocabulary, so no accent is added**). Options are the colour knobs' own labels in rack order, Micro (title), sentence case. **44px `min-block-size` and `min-inline-size` per option.** When the entry has exactly one colour knob the selector **is not rendered at all** and the caption carries that knob's label |
| **Three rails** | one per channel of the **selected** knob, sixteen detents each, using the **existing** detent-track widget: a 4px `--color-line-soft` track filled to the index in `--color-accent`, a 12px accent thumb. One `<input type="range" min="0" max="15" step="1">` per rail, `opacity: 0`, over the painted detents, filling the 44px box, with a real `<label for>`. Arrow keys step one, `Home`/`End` jump to the ends, double-click resets |
| **Detent fills** | each detent on the R rail is painted in the exact RGB444 colour that index would produce **given the current G and B**, and it re-paints when either other rail moves. The rail is a gradient made of sixteen **discrete, storable, reachable** colours — legal under A-09 precisely because every cell is a flat fill of a stored value rather than a CSS gradient |
| **Cheap-step ticks** | a 2px `--color-line` tick 4px beneath the detents whose literal is short — `0`, `17`, `85`, `170`, `255` and their one- to three-digit neighbours. **The same shape as the default marker**, so it is already a learned mark |
| **Unaffordable detents** | a real `disabled` option painted in `--color-ground` with a 1px `--color-line-soft` hairline: **absent as a colour, present as a position.** No adjacent reason line — the meter two centimetres away is the cause (X-17's precedent, and 05.1's disabled-chip precedent) |
| **The result** | a **9×9 miniature of the pad running this entry's own animation**, with every colour knob at its current value. One pad, not one per knob, because the pad is the entry. Not a rectangle. One more `PadCanvas`, gated by the same `IntersectionObserver` |
| **Caption** | `COLOUR` (6), Micro, `--color-ink-quiet`. On a single-colour entry the knob's own label sits beside it as the Micro (title) sub-label the rack already renders |
| **Accessible names** | each rail's visually-hidden label is `Red, 16 steps` / `Green, 16 steps` / `Blue, 16 steps` (13 / 15 / 14), prefixed by the selected knob's label when a selector is present (`Mute red, 16 steps`). The selector's group label is `Which colour` (12). **The composed value is announced by the group's `aria-valuetext` as the three stored integers, never as a hex** |
| **Floor** | 44px per rail, inherited free |

**The two amendments this plan makes, both by name:**

- **X-05 / X-06.** Widget selection is "chosen by `kind` and by `n`, never per configuration". **The
  `colour` kind is now chosen by `kind` alone**, because `n = 4096` would fall through to a single
  detent track — exactly the picker that lies. `colour` renders three rails and a result pad at any
  `n`; every other kind is unchanged, and `view.spec.ts` asserts that no other mapping moved.
- **TUNE-01.** "Three to six knobs" counts **knobs**, not controls. The `colour` kind is one knob
  presented as three rails.

**The counted selector split**, to be reconciled rather than assumed. From the shipped tree: six of the
nine presets carry one colour knob; `faders`, `dial` and `tpad` carry none. Twenty-five hand-authored
entries carry forty-five between them — `console`, `forge` and `strip` three each, fourteen entries two
each, eight entries one each; `cull` and `quadrant` none. **So 14 entries render no selector, 14
render two options and 3 render three, and 5 entries have no picker at all.** Recount and state.

**The guard's measured status, from 10-08.** The dearest colour on the dearest colour-bearing preset
leaves **268** characters free, and **zero of Pass B's 24,576 states crosses 908**. So on today's shelf
**no detent is ever unaffordable**. The honest sentence is not "a colour picker where some colours are
greyed out because they cost too many characters" but **"a colour picker that can say that, and that
today never has to."** The machinery still ships because it makes the claim *checkable*, because the
Lua route's hand-authored templates have far less headroom, and because the catalog grows.
