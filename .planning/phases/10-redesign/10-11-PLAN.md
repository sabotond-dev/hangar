<objective>
`MIX TWO` — genetic exploration exists in synths and in no controller-configuration editor, and the
state model here is already index vectors, so it is small.

Wave 11. Blocked on 10-10: the picker's one result canvas is part of the budget this plan spends.

**The idea.** Two parents, four children, one button. `child[k] = coin() ? a[k] : b[k]`, with one
mutation: a single `surpriseIndices` draw on one knob. Clicking a child makes it the state and the
previous state becomes `THAT ONE`, so the next mix has a parent without any bookkeeping the visitor
can see.

**Three constraints that make it cheap.**

1. **Zero new reachable states.** Every child is a point in the same space Pass A already sweeps, so
   **the sweep does not move and no budget claim changes.** Assert it rather than assume it: a child's
   index vector is componentwise within every knob's option range, and re-running the sweep after this
   plan reproduces 10-08's totals exactly.
2. **Same entry only.** Crossover between entries with different knob sets is meaningless. `THAT ONE`
   is a candidate from the last `SURPRISE ME` or a **pasted link**, decoded by the stamp machinery that
   already exists — so a shared link is a parent, which is a nice thing to be able to say and costs
   nothing.
3. **Held knobs are never crossed and never mutated.** T1 from 10-09 is what makes this behave the way
   a person expects: lock the colour you love, mix everything else.

**The copy discipline, which is a real constraint here.** *No genetics metaphor reaches the interface.
Not "breed", not "parent", not "mutate", not "DNA".* Two candidates, four results, one button labelled
`MIX TWO`. The house style's plain sentences win the fight with the metaphor, and that is a good fight
to have — assert it with a forbidden-word scan over the rendered strings.

**And no tear.** §8.4 retired the reroll firing by name: the tune panel is a **Product** surface, and a
`clip-path` on it would translate and clip its own text. The children arrive on a 160 ms opacity fade
and nothing else. `aesthetic.spec.ts` scan 3's allowlist stays closed, and this plan asserts that the
new component is **not** on it.

Purpose: TUNE-04, the second of the two ideas the survey could not find in the category.
Output: one pure module, one component, four live minis, zero new states, zero new accent uses.
</objective>
