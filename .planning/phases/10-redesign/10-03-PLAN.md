Wave 3. Blocked on 10-02: Inter is the shipped face, so the metrics `CH_PER_LINE` was measured
against are the metrics rendering now.

D-08 asked for the unnecessary text to go. `10-UI-SPEC.md` §3.1 closes that at **ten**, by a rule
rather than by a list:

> A string is retired only when the control beside it, or the pixels beside it, already say the same
> thing. **A string that names a risk, a consequence, a way back or a next step is never retired**,
> however long it is.

Nine of the ten land here. The tenth, `RESTS_DARK_NOTE`, belongs with the demonstration touch that
makes it false, and lands in 10-05.

The hard half is not the deleting. It is that **every named string is asserted character-for-character
and has a sizing twin reserving space for it**, so deleting text is a layout change in every state.
Two of the three longest strings on the site are in the header note's two cells, and retiring both
collapses **152px to 24px** — the largest single reduction in the phase. That number, and four caps,
and three more reservations, are `CH_PER_LINE`'s arithmetic. 10-01 measured it. This plan spends it.

**Open item 1, planned as recommended with its reversal costed.** The user's fork is what carries
SAFE-01 once `SAFE_PROMISE` (88, `session-copy.spec.ts:298`, `REQUIREMENTS.md:169`) goes. This plan
ships **form 1**: `SAFE_NOTE` = `Nothing is written without a click.` (**35**), Micro (title),
`--color-ink` at 9.26:1, 8px beneath the primary, above the honesty slot, **unconditional, never
swapped, never a twin**, on the chosen panel and — when the panel is closed — beneath the header's
device slot, so `panelOwnsProse` governs it exactly as it governs the two lines it already governs.
The reversals, both one edit: **form 2** moves the 88-character sentence into the device disclosure
and deletes `SAFE_NOTE` and its two render sites; **form 3** deletes it and writes a dated retirement
note into `REQUIREMENTS.md` instead. Write both into the SUMMARY with their exact costs.
