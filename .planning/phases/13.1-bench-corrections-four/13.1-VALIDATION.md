## Why the phase is serial, and the order it runs in

**The phase runs SERIAL: 02 -> 04 -> 01 -> 03 -> 05 -> 06 -> 07 -> 08.** The `wave` field of every
plan is that order (1 to 8) and every plan's `depends_on` names its immediate predecessor, so the
executor never runs two plans at once; the SUBSTANTIVE dependencies (02 -> 05 -> 06 -> 07 -> 08;
04 -> 07; 01 -> 08; 03 -> 08) are stated in each plan's header beside the order. The first draft
claimed the four "wave 1" plans pairwise disjoint; the plan check (section 4) showed they are not:
`layout.ts` is in 01 (`INTRO_*`) and 03 (`SANDBOX_HANDLE_HIT`); all four append `13.1-COPY-NEW.md`
(02 now creates it, being first) and edit STATE.md by script; and beyond files there is one
`build/`, one port 4173 with `reuseExistingServer: true`, one `test-results/`, `artifacts.e2e.ts`
reading `HEAD` (no commit while any chunk runs), layer B refusing a stale build, and 0.05-1.33 GB
free for one `--maxWorkers=2` run (13-20). Two plans at a time would be a race on all of it. The
order puts the two +0 plans first (their carried pair is the baseline), then +1, then +2.
