## `MidiField.svelte`, as shipped

**The mapping to the index.** The field is `<input type="text" inputmode="numeric"
autocomplete="off">` whose value is `refused ?? knob.readout ?? ""` - the knob's own literal as
`integerReadout` prints it (X-08), or the refused text while one is held. On input the text is
trimmed; if it is not `/^-?[0-9]+$/` the field is refused with `TYPE_A_NUMBER`; else
`typedIndex(knob.literals, text)` (view.ts, the other direction of `integerReadout`: compared as a
number, so `074` finds `74`; never clamped or snapped, so `99` on Arc finds nothing) gives the
index, and `onchange(knob.id, index)` is the region's `changeKnob` - the same call a rail makes, so
the stamp, the forecast machinery and the sweep see an index and nothing else. No knob's value count
moves; `widgetFor` still resolves these knobs to a rail; the region simply hands the MIDI partition
(`midiKnobs`, `isMidiDestination`, unchanged) to `MidiField` instead of the rack, so `Knob.svelte`
is never asked to render them and is not edited.

**The refusal.** The refused text stays in the field with `aria-invalid="true"` and the message
under it (`midi-field-{id}-message`, in the error ink on the boundary and the sentence, never on a
button) until a keystroke validates - 13-16's shape. The message is `offeredLine(knob.id,
literals)`: for a list with gaps, `A controller number here is one of 1, 16, 20, 74 or 102.`
(CONTEXT D-09's ledgered sentence, kept - W-15); for a contiguous run, `A channel here is 0 to 15.`
on a Lua entry and `A channel here is 1 to 16.` on a preset - one builder, the noun by the knob's
id, the form by the list's shape. G.29's `A channel is 1 to 16.` is NOT reused, and the ledger row
says why. A model move from outside (the per-field reset, Reset settings, Undo randomize) clears a
held refusal, so no stale message sits over a fresh value; the tuner's `set` emits synchronously, so
the DOM value is the new literal in the same tick a valid keystroke lands.

**The cue.** A Lua entry's `channel` (the run that starts at `0`) renders `LUA_CHANNEL_CUE` beneath
the field and names it in `aria-describedby`: **`The firmware counts channels from 0; your DAW’s
channel 1 is 0 here.`** (a real apostrophe). A preset's `channel` (`1`–`16`) carries no cue. The
value shown is the firmware's literal - `0` on Arc where the PDF draws `1` - because renumbering a
value about to be written to hardware is the lie X-08 forbids; **13.1-CONTEXT question 5 stands for
the gate's bench row** and the cue is what keeps the asked state from being a silent off-by-one until
it is answered.

**The `ccBase` / `send` label.** `midiFieldLabel(knob)`: the PDF's `CC number` for id `cc`, the
PDF's `Channel` for id `channel`, the knob's own word for everything else - morph's `ccBase` reads
`CC base`, a preset's `send` reads `Send`. Chosen as the least surprising: the PDF's `CC number`
would misname a base (the knob is the first of several controllers), and the preset's `Send` is
the word its option list was written for. Question 6 in CONTEXT, shipped this way.

**The rest of the shape.** Section 7's changed-field marker (`knob.index !== knob.default`, the 6px
ink square with `FIELD_CHANGED` for a screen reader) and the per-field reset (`Reset`, named
`Reset CC number`, disabled at the default) in `Knob.svelte`'s stacked shape - a 14px label line, a
4px gap, the 44px box, the reset spanning both rows at 44 x 44 - so a field is the height of the
grid field it replaces. No lock: a MIDI destination is out of every roll already. `--font-sans` with
`tabular-nums` (no `--font-mono`, so the instrument register's mono list is unmoved), no accent, no
corner; `data-testid="midi-field-{id}"` with `data-index` and `data-changed` on the root.

**Where it renders.** `TuningRegion.svelte`'s `{#snippet midi()}`: `<div class="grid-box"
data-testid="midi-grid" data-columns={gridColumns} style:--columns={gridColumns}>` with `{#each
midiKnobs as knob (knob.id)}<MidiField {knob} onchange={changeKnob} onreset={resetKnob} />`, the
grid rule (`repeat(var(--columns, 1), minmax(0, 1fr))`, the PDF's 22px gutter) on the box itself;
D-21's `columnsFor` / ResizeObserver unchanged; `MIDI_HELPER` beneath. `KnobRack.svelte` keeps its
grid layout and forecast plumbing unedited (nothing reads them now).
