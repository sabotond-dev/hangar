## Task 1 - the intro fits the screen (D-01)

> *"I dont want the index page to be scrollable, always fit on the screen"* (bench line 1)

### What was measured first

The tree at `d317321`, built and served, at the four viewports (chromium, the scratch script
`1301/measure.mjs`): the document was **1370 / 1080** at 1920 x 1080, 1133 / 900, 1106 / 768,
1106 / 720 - the intro 937 tall at every width (57 + the hero's 681 + 44 + the strip's 107 + 48)
and **the footer 121 tall, not `FOOTER_H`'s 50** (its GPLv3 licence row is a second 44px line).
So the plan's arithmetic for the centre - `calc(100dvh - var(--header-h) - var(--footer-h))` -
would have left 71px of document scroll at every viewport however the intro inside it was
squeezed. That is deviation 1.

### What was built

**`src/routes/+layout.svelte`.** The site root carries `class:intro={fill?.variant === "intro"}`
and under it is a `100dvh` flex column: the announcer (out of flow), the shell (`flex: 1 1 0;
min-block-size: 0`, its header at its own height) and the footer at its own height; `.centre.intro`
is `flex: 1 1 0; min-block-size: 0; padding: 0; overflow: hidden; container-type: size`. No number
from `layout.ts` is in the arithmetic and `shell.spec.ts` 5's literal scan and frame table are
unchanged. Inside `@media (max-width: 1023.98px)` the root is `display: contents` again and the
centre `flex: none; overflow: visible; container-type: normal` - the stacked band and the phone
flow and may scroll (13-07's stack; D-01 is the user's rule about the desktop; D.10 still open).

**`src/lib/ui/shell/layout.ts`.** Eight constants after `SURFACE_MAX`, each with its provenance,
and a header paragraph that says how the fit works and what gives when the screen is short:

**`src/lib/ui/intro/Intro.svelte`.** The root sets the numbers as UNITLESS custom properties
(`style:--intro-fit-h={INTRO_FIT_H}` and the rest; `--intro-words-min-w` in px because it is used
as a length). Then, in one place:

```css
--intro-unit: min(1px, calc(100cqh / var(--intro-fit-h)));
--intro-squeeze: clamp(0px, calc((var(--intro-unit) - var(--intro-squeeze-from) * 1px) / (1 - var(--intro-squeeze-from))), 1px);
```

`--intro-unit` is one PDF pixel at the centre's height, capped at 1px so nothing grows past the
PDF's numbers; `--intro-squeeze` is a steeper ramp for the spacings, 1px at the PDF's height and
zero at 0.3 of it. The root is a grid of `minmax(0, 1fr) auto` (the columns, then the strip at the
foot) at `block-size: 100%`; the columns one explicit row (`minmax(0, 1fr)`) with the words track
`minmax(min(100%, var(--intro-words-min-w)), 638fr)`; every margin, padding and gap is 13-07's
number times the ramp (`padding-block-start: calc(37 * var(--intro-squeeze))`, the eyebrow's 20,
the headline's 28, the cards' 44 / 40 / 16, the have line's 20, the strip's `INTRO_STRIP_PAD` and
its items' 8); the type is the number times the unit, floored: `.headline { font-size: max(34px,
calc(60 * var(--intro-unit))) }` set locally with `app.css`'s `.type-display` untouched (D-17, said
in the comment), `.sub` `max(15px, calc(19 * unit))`, the card title through `--start-card-title:
max(18px, calc(24 * unit))`. Below 1024 the unit and the ramp are pinned at `1px` (the centre is
no container there, and `cqh` would read the viewport), the root is 13-07's flex column at
`block-size: auto`, and everything is the PDF's number.

**`src/lib/ui/intro/HeroSurface.svelte`.** The panel is a grid of `auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto`
(the label row, the stage, the caption row), stretched to the columns row, its padding and gaps
`28 * unit` and `24 * unit`. The stage is `container-type: size; display: grid; place-content:
center; min-block-size: 0`, and the square is `inline-size: min(100cqw, 100cqh); aspect-ratio: 1`
- the smaller of the column's width and whatever height the two text rows leave, with no
arithmetic on the panel's padding or gaps anywhere because the stage's own box already excludes
them. Below 1024 the stage is no container and the square is the column's width, as 13-07 built
it. The cq units inside the stage resolve against the stage, never the centre; the file says so
and nothing inside the stage reads `--intro-unit`.

**`src/lib/ui/intro/StartCard.svelte`.** `min-block-size: var(--start-card-min, 108px)`, `padding:
var(--start-card-pad, 16px) 24px`, the text gap `var(--start-card-gap, 6px)`, the title
`var(--start-card-title, 24px)` - the intro's `.cards` sets them, the PDF's values are the
fallbacks. The padding's fallback is 16, not 13-07's 20: at 20 the card rendered 112 against the
PDF's 108; at 16 the 108 minimum is what binds.

## Deviations from the plan

### 1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The intro's centre is sized by flex from the rendered chrome, not by the plan's calc

- **Found during:** Task 1, the measurement before the first edit.
- **Issue:** The plan's `.centre.intro { block-size: calc(100dvh - var(--header-h) - var(--footer-h)) }`
  assumes the footer is `FOOTER_H` (50). `Footer.svelte` is `min-block-size: var(--footer-h)` and
  its content - the brand line and the GPLv3 licence row, each a 44px line, 12px padding either
  side, a hairline - renders **121px**. The calc would leave the document 71px taller than the
  viewport at every size, and the e2e title would be red however the intro were squeezed.
- **Fix:** Under the intro variant the site root is a `100dvh` flex column and the centre is
  `flex: 1 1 0` - what the header and the footer leave, as they render - and a size container so
  the intro reads its height as `100cqh`. No constant is in the arithmetic; `INTRO_FIT_H`'s
  paragraph and the layout's header say why. The plan's `dvh` term is on the site root; the
  intro's unit reads `cqh`; the unit test asserts what was built.
- **Files:** `src/routes/+layout.svelte`, `src/lib/ui/intro/Intro.svelte`, `layout.ts`; commit `861b53b`.
- **Handed on:** the same calc sizes the app frame (`.frame`), which is 71px short by the same

  `/playground/aurora/` 930 / 720 at 1280 x 720. Not this plan's; `deferred-items.md` A.1 for the gate.
