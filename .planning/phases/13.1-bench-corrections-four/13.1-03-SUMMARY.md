## Task 1 - `resizeSelectedTo` and the palette (`cabb827`)

**`editor.ts`.** `PALETTE` is four RGB444 triples with the comment saying which two are the page's
and which two are proposed; `DEFAULT_COLOUR` is `PALETTE[0]` (the template, the picker's reset
and `RegionInspector` read it). A private `created` counter indexes `PALETTE[created % 4]` in
`nextColour()` and then increments - so the first element is `PALETTE[0]`; `newRegion` takes an
optional colour and asks `nextColour()` only when none is handed in, so the cap's probe (which
builds a throwaway button) passes `DEFAULT_COLOUR` and never advances the cycle; `place()` and
`template()` give the entry back when `addRegion` refuses; `load()` seeds `created` from the
loaded count. `resizeSelectedTo(box)` refuses silently in Play or with nothing selected, returns
without an entry when the box is the region's own, otherwise `applyPatch({col,row,w,h},
"resize", dragKey(id))` then `history.seal()`, clears a stale field problem and moves the focus
to the box's origin on success, and returns `applyEdit`'s problem on a refusal with the surface
untouched. The header's section 1 gains the drag and the palette paragraphs.

**`sandbox-ui.spec.ts`.** Test 2's clause inverted: exactly one method starting `resize`, none
matching `/pointer|hover/i`, the pointermove slice never calling `onclick(` or `onresize(`. Test
7 (+1): the accepted box, a second drag a second entry, undo / redo re-selecting, the overlap
line naming Button 1 with the surface the same object, the off-surface line, a knob to 2 x 2
refused with the knob's line, the same box no entry, Play and no selection silent, every emitted
surface valid. Test 8 (+1): the four kinds carry `PALETTE[0..3]`, the fifth wraps, a deletion is
not a step back, the picker still recolours, a refused placement does not advance, a surface

## Task 2 - the plate (`9b2303b`, with the refusal fix in `9f49c6e`)

**The drag.** Each of the eight handles is named (`data-handle` nw n ne w e sw s se, the existing
order) and gets an invisible 16 x 16 hit rect beneath it - `SANDBOX_HANDLE_HIT` in `layout.ts`
with the reason (8 is the page's drawn size; 44 over a corner would swallow the four cells around
it; 16 keeps the cell clickable) - under `data-testid="surface-handle-hit"`, never
`surface-handle` (W-07): the counts of eight in `sandbox.e2e.ts` and `radius.e2e.ts` stand.
`startDrag`: `stopPropagation()` so the plate's own pointerdown (the click path) does not run,
focus the plate without scrolling, `setPointerCapture` on the plate, remember the handle and the
region's box. `onpointermove` still reads `hover = cellOf(event)` and, with a drag live, sets
`dragBox = boxFor(handle, from, hover)` - the held edges follow the cell, the others are the
anchor, `min` / `max` keep the box at least 1 x 1; `proposed` renders `dragBox` first, so the
dashed rect 13-16 draws is the drag's proposal. `onpointerup` (and `pointercancel`) releases the
capture and calls `endDrag`: the same box commits nothing; otherwise `onresize(box)` and the
returned problem's message is held with the surface it was refused against, and the status line
shows it while `view.surface` is still that object - until the next pointerdown or keydown on
the plate clears it, or an Undo, a field or the list moves the surface. **Pointer capture makes
the moves land wherever the pointer goes**, which is what carried the e2e's first drag to a cell
under the app frame's footer. Cursors per handle (`nwse` / `nesw` / `ns` / `ew-resize`), `move`
on the plate while held; `touch-action: none` was already on the plate, so a finger drags the
same. Play renders no handles (unchanged). The header restates NO DRAG IS EVER REQUIRED and
describes the drag as the third route.

**Own colour.** Nothing on the plate: `fillOf(r)` reads the region's colour as before.
