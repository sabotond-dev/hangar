### The four-name carry-forward block

The chain above only works if the name a plan reaches for is actually in the SUMMARY it reaches into,
and for e2e it was not. **05.1-05 reads 05.1-04's SUMMARY and 05.1-10 reads 05.1-09's, and neither
05.1-04 nor 05.1-09 runs Playwright** — so a rule of "read the previous SUMMARY's e2e total" pointed
at nothing three times out of eleven. The fix is mechanical rather than clever:

> **Every SUMMARY in this phase carries the same four names, whether or not that plan moved them.**

| Name | What it is | How it moves |
|---|---|---|
| `BASE_FILES` | the `test:quick` **file** count as that plan left the tree | re-measured by every plan that changes it; copied verbatim otherwise |
| `BASE_TESTS` | the `test:quick` **test** count as that plan left the tree | same |
| `BASE_SWEEP` | the literal `3 13` | never re-derived; the `sweep` project is a named-file include and this phase adds no file to it |
| `PREV_E2E` | the **last measured** Playwright total, with the plan that measured it named beside it | copied verbatim by every plan that does not run `test:e2e`; re-measured and re-stated by every plan that does |

A plan's `PREV_FILES` / `PREV_TESTS` are the immediately preceding SUMMARY's `BASE_FILES` /
`BASE_TESTS`. **Three plans run `test:e2e`** — 05.1-05, 05.1-10 and 05.1-11 — and the other eight
carry `PREV_E2E` unchanged. Every plan states the carry as an acceptance criterion on its last task,
and 05.1-10 is instructed to **stop** rather than guess if the name is missing from 05.1-09's SUMMARY.

`BASE_CHECK` (the `svelte-check` file count) is recorded once, in 05.1-01, as provenance only. It is
carried nowhere and asserted nowhere; only `0 errors` is ever asserted, because the file count moves
whenever a file is added.

### Facts the map depends on

Counted or measured in this repository on 2026-09-04 by the researcher and re-read by the planner from
the sources. They are reproductions, not estimates.

- **Sixteen catalog entries. Eight in `FRONT_DOOR`; eight excluded — and the excluded eight are
  `tpad` plus the SEVEN Lua entries.** There are seven Lua-sourced configurations, not eight. Any plan
  copy that says eight produces an off-by-one gate.
- **`$lib/catalog` is heavy because of exactly one line.** `src/lib/catalog/entries/ported.ts:13`
  imports `presetById` from `src/vendor/botor/_pad.ts`, which imports `@intechstudio/grid-protocol` at
  module scope — a **131,101-byte** chunk. `types.ts`'s only `_pad` import is `import type`, which is
  erased. So a prerendered page that statically imports `$lib/catalog` puts the compiler on its first
  paint, and `src/lib/catalog/front-door.ts` is the shipped answer to exactly that.
- **GHOST lies under the shipped motion derivation.** `front-door.spec.ts`'s `deriveMotion` is
  `any animating → "animated"`, and `frames.json` records GHOST as `animating: true` at **every**
  sampled tick with **zero** lit bytes at every sampled tick. `FidelityLine.svelte` suppresses the
  quiet line for animated entries, so the naive derivation produces an unexplained black square. The
  listing derives `restsBlack → "dark"` **first** (D-14).
- **Three shipped descriptions carry an ASCII apostrophe, and only two of them can be fixed at
  source.** `entries/ghost.ts` (`finger's`) and `entries/morph.ts` (`corner's`) are HANGAR's and are
  corrected to U+2019 in **plan 05.1-05** — together with `src/lib/catalog/listing.ts`, in **one
  commit**, because `listing.spec.ts` test 1 asserts strict equality with `byId(id)` and correcting
  one without the other is red. Radar's sentence is **vendored** (`src/vendor/botor/_pad.ts:4255`,
  mirrored byte-equal in `front-door.ts` and the listing) and is **never edited**:
  `src/lib/fidelity/vendored-diff.spec.ts` reconstructs the pristine upstream bytes and compares a
  sha256 against a pinned manifest. It is handled at render time by `typographic()`
  (`src/lib/browse/typographic.ts`, plan 05.1-02), wired at exactly two sites — `Coverflow.svelte`'s
  `sr-only` span (05.1-04) and `CatalogCard.svelte`'s visible description (05.1-06) — and **not** at
  `src/routes/c/[id]/+page.svelte`'s `meta` and `og:description`, which stay byte-equal to source
  because `e2e/first-experience.e2e.ts` and `og/build.spec.ts` test 4 read them.
- **Eight entries are featured and eight are not.** Counted on 2026-09-04: ARC, CHORUS, EUCLID,
  LATTICE, MORPH, Aurora, Pinwheel, Nine Pads. The card's reserved `FEATURED` band therefore holds
  the word on eight cards and nothing on eight.
- **The fidelity sentence is 231 characters**, measured from `src/lib/ui/FidelityLine.svelte`. Plan
  05.1-08 lifts it to `src/lib/ui/fidelity-line.ts` unchanged.
- **41 tags over 16 entries; 32 of them on exactly one entry.** The nine with a count of two or more
  are `playable` (4), `generative` (3), `gestural` (3), `hypnotic` (3), `ambient`, `colour`, `drums`,
  `expressive`, `readable` (2 each).
- **`addedAt` has exactly two distinct values** — nine ported entries at `2026-09-02`, seven Lua
  entries at `2026-09-04`. "Newest" is two blocks, not a ranking, so **every sort needs an explicit
  final tiebreak** and the e2e assertion for Newest must be the **block boundary**, never a strict
  sixteen-element sequence.
- **A one-entry ring is well defined.** Read from `src/lib/coverflow/slots.ts` and evaluated at
  `count = 1`: `visibleWindow(c, r, 1)` is `[0]`, `step(0, ±1, 1)` is `0`, `slotOffset(0, 0, 1)` is
  `0`.
- **Kit 2.70.3, from its own source.** `respond.js:433-434` and `load_data.js:71` call
  `disable_search(url)` when prerendering, and `utils/url.js:174-188` makes `url.search` and
  `url.searchParams` **throw**. `client.js:2551-2581` (`replaceState`) sets `page.state` and never
  `page.url`; `client.js:2871-2905` (popstate, shallow) *does* call `update_url`. `client.js:2045-2048`
  runs `restore_snapshot()` **after** `after_navigate_callbacks`, which is why `export const snapshot`
  is the wrong tool for anything that changes layout height.
- **`src/routes/+layout.ts` already sets `prerender = true` and `trailingSlash = "always"` for the
  whole site**, and `vite.config.ts` sets `prerender.entries: ["*"]`. `/browse/` therefore needs **no
  `+page.ts`**, which is also the only way to be certain nothing on that route ever reads
  `url.searchParams` during prerender.
- **`buildTuner`'s `destroy()` closes the engine it published.** `swapEngine` transfers ownership at
  `onpreview` and closes only the previous engine; `destroy()` contradicts it. Harmless while no Lua
  entry has a solo page; fatal the moment `/c/euclid/` exists.
- **gen-og for sixteen costs nothing.** Measured: all sixteen entries build an engine, run to
  `OG_TICK` 64 and PNG-encode in **382 ms**; the whole script including Vite's SSR boot is **2,155 ms**.
- **The e2e arithmetic.** 39 `test()` declarations across eight files; the `webkit-phone` project
  re-runs only the five `@webkit`-tagged titles, so 39 + 5 = 44. **A new title with `@webkit` adds 2;
  without it, 1.**

### The design decision that shapes every count

**Every gate in this phase loops over its entries internally and never uses `it.each`.** A test names
the offending entry in its assertion message rather than being one test per entry. So a configuration
added in a later phase changes no test count here, and a green run means the same thing before and
after the catalog grows. It is also why widening `og/build.spec.ts` from eight entries to sixteen
leaves it at five tests.

### Why the waves are serial

No two plans in this phase run concurrently, and the reason is arithmetic rather than caution: every
acceptance criterion asserts an **exact cumulative test count**, and two plans adding specs in the same
wave would both compute the wrong total. Serial waves cost wall time and buy a gate that cannot be
green for the wrong reason. The `wave` numbers in the plan frontmatter are dependency groupings, one
plan each.

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** `npm run test:quick`, plus `npm run lint` for tasks touching
  HANGAR-owned files, plus `npm run check` for tasks touching `.svelte` or `.ts` — and **always** for
  a task that changes a component's props, since the `row`, `arrows` and structural `{ id, name }`
  changes surface type errors across five call sites.
- **After every plan wave:** `npm run check && npm run lint && npm run test:quick && npm run test:sweep`.
- **After any task that changes what a build contains:** `npm run build`, then `test:quick` **again**,
  because `config-shape.spec.ts` test 14 and `og/build.spec.ts` tests 1, 4 and 5 read `build/` and are
  only armed when it is fresh.
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** the full suite including `npm run build` and `npm run test:e2e`
  against the **production static build** — plan 05.1-11 runs it as its last task.
- **Max feedback latency:** ~35 s (quick), ~180 s (wave, with the sweep), ~2 min (e2e with the build).

## What the fixtures and gates prove

- `listing.spec.ts` proves the restatement agrees with `CATALOG` field by field and that the motion
  derivation is the `restsBlack`-first one. It does **not** prove the three authored `quiet` sentences
  are good copy — it proves they exist, are single-line, and are the one shared sentence for the three
  dark entries.
- `sort.spec.ts` proves the three orders are total and agree with `$lib/catalog`'s own three on the
  id sequence, and that no locale-aware comparison is used. It does **not** prove the resulting order
  is the one a designer would choose: the all-caps-before-mixed-case consequence is a **recorded
  finding**, and `05.1-UI-SPEC.md` morning question 4 is the user's to answer.
- `filter.spec.ts` proves the intersection semantics and derives the nine standing chips from the
  data. The `MORE TAGS` disclosure in `05.1-UI-SPEC.md` W-19 is **not built** — D-15 is binding and
  says the 32 singletons stay searchable rather than becoming chips. An **active** single tag still
  renders a removable chip, because "individually removable" requires it.
- `typographic.spec.ts` proves the transform on the two HANGAR sentences as **literals** (so it goes
  on covering them after 05.1-05 corrects the source) and on Radar's sentence **read from the
  catalog** (so it goes on proving the transform fires against shipped, uneditable data). It does
  **not** prove the transform is applied at any render site — that is 05.1-04's and 05.1-06's
  acceptance criteria, and `e2e/browse.e2e.ts` test 1 seeing Radar's card.
- `browse-ui.spec.ts` proves what the screen refuses to do: no popularity word in any rendered string,
  no `--color-over`, no hex literal, no listbox or grid role, one live region, a 16px field, 44px
  controls — **and that the count is said three ways**: a visible `aria-hidden` line, an
  always-present hidden expansion carrying `Showing {n} of {total} configurations.` with neither
  `aria-live` nor `aria-hidden`, and the one live region. The expansion is the element a visitor
  arriving on `/browse/?q=ghost` depends on, because no change event has fired and the live region
  has nothing to say. Its needles are assembled from fragments so the spec does not contain the words it forbids.
- `config-shape.spec.ts` tests 13 and 14 are the two guards that keep `/browse/` light, and **neither
  could see it before this phase**: the markers do not match `$lib/catalog` and the artefact scan did
  not include `build/browse/index.html`. Both widenings are proven by mutation in 5.1-08-02.
- `e2e/browse.e2e.ts` test 7 proves the WebAssembly claim **as stated**: *a visitor who never brings a
  Lua card into view never downloads the VM*. It is tested with `?q=aurora` because the default
  Featured sort puts Lua cards in the first screenful, so a bare cold `/browse/` legitimately fetches
  the VM and a test that forbade it would be asserting the wrong thing.
- `e2e/browse.e2e.ts` test 6 **records** the two-second paint count for sixteen cards. It is not a
  threshold. `.planning/research/STACK.md`'s 10-16 / 30+ estimate is explicitly unverified and this
  phase does not promote it to a gate.
- `e2e/browse-webkit.e2e.ts` test 2 **asserts** that a still, lit configuration is still lit after a
  sort, and records the pixel counts. It asserts rather than merely records because
  `SimHost.repaintAll()` makes the outcome deterministic; the numbers are kept because the underlying
  question — whether a canvas bitmap survives a re-parenting move on WebKit — is not otherwise settled.

## Standing hazards carried into this phase

- **The coverflow's curation does not move.** No plan may add a Lua entry to `FRONT_DOOR` to make a
  demo work: `front-door.spec.ts` asserts every row entry has `preview === "padsim"`, and the decision
  is the user's (Phase 8 D-18, CONTEXT open question 2).
- **The default Featured order opens the catalog on hand-authored configurations**, because
  featured-first then name puts the all-caps Lua names ahead of the mixed-case ported ones. That is
  D-10 as amended working exactly as written; it is a data and curation question, and it is recorded
  rather than quietly re-sorted.
- **Three of sixteen cards are black at rest**, and one of them (GHOST) reports `animating: true` at
  every sampled tick. The listing's derivation is the only thing standing between that fact and an
  unexplained black square.
- **`buildTuner`'s `destroy()` was closing a published engine.** Fixed in 05.1-04 and proven twice —
  in node and in a browser. Any future engine handed across a component boundary inherits the rule:
  **ownership transfers at the handover**.
- **`page.url` is asymmetric** — stale after `replaceState`, fresh after popstate. Nothing in this
  phase may read it outside the one-time seed.
- **The e2e total is tag-sensitive.** A `@webkit` tag added to a title in the wrong file moves the
  suite total by one in a way no delta table explains.
