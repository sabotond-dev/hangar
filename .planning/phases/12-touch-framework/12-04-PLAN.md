makes that cursor more visible, not less. A visitor who filters `still` and then taps LUMEN sees a
cell move. That is the doubt, in one sentence, and the guard stands: **if it reads as a stretch to
whoever executes this, stop and say so before editing.**

against a floor of six, so *some* card must join it or the floor moves - and the floor is not to be

already carries `still` and less LUMEN). Any of them re-homed onto `still` closes the arithmetic;
which one is CONT-03 taste and therefore the user's, not the executor's. **Stop and ask; do not pick a
second card silently, and do not weaken the floor.**

## `LEGACY_TAG_MAP`, which breaks in a way the first draft of this plan got backwards

each verified before anything is edited:

1. **`keys` is not one of the fifty-five.** `RETIRED_VOCABULARY` (`facets.ts:180`) holds `harmonic`
   and `hotkeys`; it does **not** hold `keys`. `keys` was minted at the 10-06 re-cut as the
   destination of `harmonic`, so it was never a shipped `?tag=`. Clause 2 is about *retired shipped
   tags*, so `keys` is not eligible for a row at all.

   in vocabulary.legacy)`. A `?for=` value is matched against `vocabulary.for` and nothing else. So a
   `keys` row could not migrate a `?for=keys` link even if it existed.
