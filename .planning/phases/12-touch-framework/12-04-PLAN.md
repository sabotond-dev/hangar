<objective>
Removals ten, eleven and twelve, and the two facet rules they break.

> `LATTICE: remove` · `FORGE: remove` · `SHUTTLE: REMOVE`

No diagnosis is needed and none is offered. The removal comes before the fixes for the reason 11-01
gave: `frames.json`, `static/og/` and `wild-stamps.json` are keyed by id, and every later plan that
regenerates one of them should do so over twenty-six rows rather than twenty-nine.

**Both rules 11-01 warned about break here, and the precedent is D-01.** After the three go, `keys`
holds one entry (CHORUS) against a spec that asserts zero singletons (`facets.spec.ts:231`), and
`still` holds five against a FEELS floor of six (`:210`). D-01's answer to the same arithmetic was to
retire the thin term and re-home the survivor onto a word that describes how the card feels - never to
weaken a rule that was raised deliberately. So: **retire `keys`** (FOR 8 -> 7; CHORUS -> `play`, which
makes `play` three: nine drum pads, a snake, and nine chords under your fingers - a truthful word), and
**re-home LUMEN onto `still`** (`show, expressive, readable` -> `show, still, readable`: it paints once
and never moves, and `expressive` stays at ten). Both are judgements about the cards per CONT-03. If
either reads as a stretch to whoever executes this, stop and say so.

**And the floor sentence moves.** `facets.ts:15` and `:104` say `precise` and `still` sit at exactly
six after 11-01 and that a tenth removal breaks a FEELS rule. After this plan `still` is at six again
by re-homing and `precise` is wherever the census puts it; the header must say what the **thirteenth**
removal would break, in the file whose spec would go red.

Purpose: three reports closed, the vocabulary kept honest a second time, and every fixture smaller
before the fixes regenerate them.
Output: three entry files deleted, twenty-three files edited, a catalog of `BASE_CATALOG - 3`.
