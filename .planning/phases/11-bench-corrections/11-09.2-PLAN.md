## The three hypotheses, and each one is measured or disproved rather than carried

**Name all three in the SUMMARY, with the verdict and the evidence for each.** A shrug is not a
verdict.

### H1 - the knob does not reach the emitted Lua

`@DEPTH` reaches **exactly one site** in the source: `local d=36-n//9*@DEPTH` inside `F(n)`. `renderLua`
substitutes by plain `String.replaceAll`.

**Measure it, do not read it.** Render LUMEN at each of the four indices and compare the four Setup
strings. Expect four distinct strings differing at exactly one character, and **count the occurrences
of the substituted value** to prove the one-site claim rather than restating the header.

### H2 - the knob reaches the Lua but not the LED bytes

**This is the hypothesis the whole plan exists for, and it is the one the checkpoint's own numbers do
not actually address.** The **78 % / 11 %** figures are the arithmetic `d/36`. They are **not** a
reading of an emitted frame. Between the arithmetic and a lit LED sit `glc`'s colour slot, `glp`'s
phase, `shapeIntensity`, the per-layer weights, the two-layer sum and the single divide by 512 with its
clamp at 255 (`pad-sim.ts:1487-1507`).

**So read the FRAME, not the formula.** Build LUMEN's engine at each of the four `@DEPTH` values, run
it with no gesture, and print the **243-byte frame's nine rows** - the `(r, g, b)` triple of a chosen
column at each row - at each value. That is a 9 x 4 table of real emitted bytes and it answers H2
outright.

### H3 - the knob reaches the LEDs but the change is not where the user was looking

**Check this even if H2 comes back clean, because it is the hypothesis most consistent with the
words.** `d = 36 - row*@DEPTH`, so:

- **row 0 is EXACTLY the anchor at every value of @DEPTH.** The top row cannot move. Ever.
- the arithmetic ratios per row, from the source (verify these against the frame rather than trusting
  them here):

| row | @DEPTH 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| 1 | 97 | 94 | 92 | 89 |
| 2 | 94 | 89 | 83 | 78 |
| 4 | 89 | 78 | 67 | 56 |
| 8 | 78 | 56 | 33 | 11 |

- **and the DEFAULT is index 2, `@DEPTH = "3"`** - already the second-deepest. A user turning the knob
  **one step** from where it ships moves the bottom row from 33 % to 22 % or to 42 % of the anchor, and
  the top three rows by a handful of LED counts.

**If the frame confirms H2 is clean, H3 is the live answer and the deliverable changes shape**: the
complaint is about the ramp's SHAPE - a knob whose whole visible effect is in the bottom third of the
pad - rather than about its depth. Say that in those words. **The user asked for the ramp to be
deepened and the honest deepening may be a different curve rather than a bigger multiplier**; cost both
and put the shortfall in front of them rather than trimming the ask.

### H4, named and disproved from evidence already in the tree rather than measured

*"Writing the Setup config does not make the module re-run it, so the LEDs never repaint until a power
cycle."* This would explain *"no difference in the LEDs"* on **every Setup-only card**, and LUMEN is
the most Setup-only card in the catalog - `timer: ""`, nothing runs after the field is painted.

**It is disproved by evidence the user already has**: the rest of this bench report is a list of
observations about cards visibly doing things after install. If a written Setup never ran, the whole
bench sheet would be a single note. **State it and dismiss it with that reason** - it is worth thirty
seconds of prose to stop the next reader spending a bench cycle on it, and `docs/INSTALL-RUNBOOK.md`
is where a real answer would live if one is ever wanted.
