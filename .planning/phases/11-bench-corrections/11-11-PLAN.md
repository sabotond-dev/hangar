## What GHOST is, and what the redesign owes it

Read `ghost.ts` in full before deleting a line of it, including its header. The card is a
record-and-replay gesture pad: a finger's path is captured, then played back as a ghost. Its `listing`
line and its `quiet` line describe that promise to a visitor, and **the promise is not what the user
asked to change** — the behaviour is.

**Three things the new design must answer, drawn from the note rather than invented:**

1. ***"Doesn't work reliably."*** The most likely mechanical causes are now closed: a fast tap
   produced nothing (class B) and crossed cells never went dark (class A). **State whether the new
   design's reliability rests on those fixes or on something else**, because "we redesigned it and
   also fixed two class bugs" leaves nobody able to say which mattered.
2. ***"The LED colors the pad."*** Read as: the card leaves the pad coloured when it should not.
   11-02-03's **matched-pair residue probe** is the machine statement of that complaint, and the new
   GHOST must pass it — a gesture must leave no cell lit that a never-touched run leaves dark.
3. ***"Resetting is not reliable."*** The old reset is a second-finger contact
   (`if i>0 then if e==4 then s.g={} ... end end`). A second finger is undiscoverable and, on a pad
   with no modifier, easily mistimed. **Design the reset as a deliberate, visible gesture** and say
   what it is. A held press on a marked cell, a two-cell corner, a dedicated row — **cost at least two
   and state the choice**, the same discipline 11-09 applied to STAGE's selection gesture.

## Constraints the design must respect, not discover

**`restsBlack` is a fixture-proved property, not a declaration.** `frames.spec.ts` asserts
`nonZeroBytes` agrees with each entry's `restsBlack` **in both directions**. If the new GHOST rests
lit, `restsBlack` becomes false and `demo.ts`'s `DARK_BY_CONSTRUCTION`-adjacent handling and
`gen-og.mjs`'s dark-card path both change with it. **Re-derive it from the regenerated fixture; do not
carry the old value forward.**

**`motion` is the same kind of property.** `front-door.ts` says motion must never be guessed or
aspirational, and the engine reports `host.animating || host.timerArmed` — so **any entry with a
stored Timer classifies as `animated` whatever its picture is doing.** GHOST has a Timer today. If the
redesign keeps one, it is `animated`; if it drops one, read the fixture rather than reasoning about it.

**The demo path at `demo.ts:177` must exercise the new gesture.** It drives the OG image and the
browse card's motion, and a demo path that replays the *old* gesture on the *new* card produces a
social preview of nothing happening. Rewrite it with the redesign, in the same commit.

**Its tags.** `listing.ts` gives GHOST one `FOR` and two `FEELS` per CONT-03. If the redesign changes
what the card is for, the tags move — and **the vocabulary is closed at eight and six**, with
`precise` and `still` sitting **exactly at the FEELS floor of 6** after 11-01. **A tag moved off
either of those breaks a health rule**, so check the histogram before changing a tag, not after.

## The budget, and the honest way to spend it

Setup 305 / 601 free. Timer 333 / 573 free. **Cost the design before writing it** — sketch the Lua,
measure it, and only then author. D-04 says blank-page entries are planned as authoring work with the
budget costed up front, and the reason is visible three entries away: SNAKE's Timer has **36
characters free**, which is why "one note per movement" could not be added to it and why the user's
deferral was fortunate rather than merely convenient.

**If the design does not fit, that is a finding**: report the shortfall in characters and what would
have to go, rather than quietly shipping a smaller idea and calling it the redesign.
