was. **Get that wrong and it is two faders in different colours.**

The spring-back is also the visual — on release, pitch should **travel home rather than snap**, in
light and in MIDI together — and that needs the Timer, which is why this entry spends **both** budgets

## Verified against the host before this was proposed

- **Pitch bend is reachable.** MIDI pitch bend is status **224** (`0xE0`), 14-bit, centre **8192**,
  sent as `self:gms(<ch>, 224, <lsb>, <msb>, 0)`. The existing entries already send `144` (note on),
  `128` (note off) and `176` (CC) through the same call, so the status byte is **not special-cased
  anywhere** — it is data.
- **`gmbs` is NOT pitch bend.** It is one of the compiler's HID out-calls — `gmms`, `gmbs`, `gks` are
  mouse-move, mouse-button and keyboard, recorded by `recordHid` in `lua-host.ts:458` and bound bare
  only because tpad's compiled Setup opens with `gmbs(3,0)`. **Anything reading `gmbs` as "bend send"
  would produce a config that silently emits mouse clicks.** Assert its absence from this entry.
- **The mod wheel is ordinary CC.** CC1 is the modulation wheel by convention; `176` with controller
  `1`.

## The five questions `WHEELS-REQUEST.md` leaves to whoever plans it

**1. The budget.** This entry spends Setup **and** Timer, 908 each. Two independent wheels on one pad
plus a spring-back animation is not obviously cheap. **Cost it before designing it**, the way this
phase costs every other request — sketch the Lua, measure it, then author. If the spring animation does
not fit alongside two wheels, **that is a finding**: report the shortfall and what would have to go,
rather than shipping a snap-back and calling it a spring.

**2. Where the divide falls.** Nine columns do not halve. **Columns 0-3 and 5-8 with column 4 as a lit
divider is the obvious answer and it is honest about the geometry; 0-4 and 4-8 sharing a column is
not.** Take the honest one unless there is a measured reason not to, and say why either way. The lit
divider is also free visual language: it marks the boundary a finger must not cross and it costs one
column of paint.

**3. Resolution.** Touch gives `y` in 0..127 while the pad shows nine rows, so **the value can be far
finer than the display**. Pitch especially wants the full 14 bits — **the LEDs are the readout, not the
quantiser.** Do not quantise the value to the row. This is the same distinction 11-13 drew for STRIP
and the opposite of the one 11-07 drew for CONSOLE, and the reason is the same in all three: continuous
output wants resolution, a cell grid does not.

Whether the 10-bit unlock (`self:txma(1023)self:tyma(1023)`, **+30**) is worth it here is a real
question and 11-13's answer is the precedent. Cost it; take it only for a control whose output is
genuinely continuous, which pitch is.

**4. Whether the spring returns in MIDI as well as in light.** **It must**, or a held note stays bent
after the finger is gone. **That is the failure mode worth a test** and `WHEELS-REQUEST.md` names it as
such. The joystick preset's compiled spring is the shipped precedent — its rests are **exact
literals** (`8192` for a bend axis, `64` for a CC axis) rather than scaled positions, so it lands
bit-perfect wherever the finger left. Do the same: **the last frame of the spring emits exactly 8192**,
not "close to centre".

**5. Rest state.** Pitch rests at centre — lit at the middle row. Mod rests **where it was left**,
which means it has state that survives a release, and on a fresh Setup it starts at zero. Both rest
states must be **visible on the pad at power-on**, or the card looks broken before it is touched.

## Constraints the card inherits

**Motion.** The entry stores a Timer for the spring, so it classifies as **`animated`** whatever its
picture is doing — the engine reports `host.animating || host.timerArmed`. `front-door.ts` says motion
must never be guessed or aspirational and `frames.json` proves it in both directions. **Declare
`animated` and let the fixture confirm it**, rather than the other way round.

**The Timer must not run forever.** A spring-back Timer that keeps ticking after the pitch has reached
centre is a card that never settles, and the catalog's own scheduler treats a permanently animating
entry as one that never releases its rAF slot. **Stop the Timer when the spring lands** — `gtt` with a
period, then disarmed — and assert it. A `gtt(index, 0)` to stop it is exactly the silent-stop trap, so
whatever the disarm mechanism is, **read how `pomodoro.ts` and `shuttle.ts` do it** rather than
inventing one.

**Tags, from the closed vocabulary.** One `FOR`, two `FEELS`. `modulation` is the truthful `FOR` — a
pitch and mod wheel pair is the modulation section of a keyboard — and it takes that term from 7 to 8.
For FEELS, note that **`precise` and `still` sit at exactly 6, the floor**: a wheel pair is a
reasonable `expressive`, and `precise` is defensible for a 14-bit control, which would lift that term
off the floor. **Choose what is true of the card, per CONT-03, and state the resulting histogram** —
do not pick a tag to move a number, and do not avoid a true tag because it moves one.

**Everything a new entry has to be added to.** `index.ts` (import, `CATALOG`, re-export), `listing.ts`
(a full `ListingEntry` with description, motion, quiet, tags, featured, `restsBlack`, preview),
`front-door.ts`'s `EXCLUDED_FROM_ROW` (it is not a ported preset, so it is excluded with a `why`),
`frames.json` (regenerated), `static/og/` (rebuilt), `docs/HARDWARE-AUDITION.md` (a new checklist row,
taking the count from **whatever 11-14 left** to one more — 23 → **24** under three of 11-14's four
answers and 24 → **25** under `new-entry` — renumbered contiguously, `audition.spec.ts:202` asserts
contiguity and its `ROW_COUNT` moves with the document), and `wild-stamps.json` **only if** the fixture's contract requires a record per entry —
**read `stamp.spec.ts:399` and its 18-record assertions before touching that file**, and if it does not
require one, say so rather than adding one for symmetry.
