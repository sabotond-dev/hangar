## The question the plan exists to answer, answered in bytes

> **Does turning LUMEN's depth knob change what the pad emits, and by how much,
> on which rows?**

**Yes. Four `@DEPTH` values render four DISTINCT 243-byte frames. Column 0's
bottom row walks `196,69,0` at `@DEPTH 1` to `139,49,0`, `84,29,0` and
`27,9,0` - 77 per cent of the emitted anchor down to 11 per cent of it - while
row 0 is `253,89,0` at every one of the four and cannot move. The worst channel
spread across the four values, row by row from the top, is 0, 21, 42, 63, 85,
105, 126, 148, 169.**

## The four hypotheses, each with a verdict and its evidence

### H1 - the knob does not reach the emitted Lua: **DISPROVED, by measurement**

`renderLua` at the four indices produces **four distinct Setup strings**, each
**604 characters**, differing at **exactly one character position, 173**, in the
context `...i=n%9*3 local d=36-n//9*1 local r=H[...`. `@DEPTH` occurs **once**
in the template, counted rather than restated from the header. The Timer is the
empty string at all four.

### H2 - the knob reaches the Lua but not the LED bytes: **DISPROVED, by measurement, and this is what the plan existed for**

**Four distinct 243-byte frames.** The bottom row is a strictly decreasing
function of the depth index on **every anchored channel**, per column, and the
frame is identical at tick 0 and tick 5 at every value (LUMEN's Timer is empty,
so the picture is settled the moment Setup returns). `nonZeroBytes` is a flat
**171 of 243** at all four values, so `restsBlack` stays FALSE at every value
and not only at the default.

### H3 - the knob reaches the LEDs but the change is not where the user was looking: **THE LIVE ANSWER, and it is arithmetic**

- **Row 0 is `d = 36 - 0*@DEPTH = 36` at every value and CANNOT MOVE. Ever.**
  Emitted `253,89,0` at all four. A person watching the top of the pad while
  turning the knob is **correctly reporting what the pad does**.
- The spread climbs with the row: **0, 21, 42, 63, 85, 105, 126, 148, 169**.
  Rows 1 and 2 move by 21 and 42 counts of 255 **across the knob's entire
  travel**; the bottom row moves by 169.
- **The shipped default is index 2 of 4 - already the second-deepest.** One step
  from where the card ships moves **row 1 by seven counts** (231 -> 238 or 224),
  **row 2 by fourteen**, and **the bottom row by fifty-five** (84 -> 139 or 27).
  The knob's full travel is only available from a position the card does not
  ship at.

**So the complaint is about the ramp's SHAPE - a knob whose whole visible effect
is in the lower half of the pad - rather than about its depth.** Said in the
plan's own words, and it is the reading most consistent with an observation of
*no difference*.

### H4 - a written Setup never runs, so the LEDs never repaint until a power cycle: **DISMISSED from evidence already in the tree, not measured**

This would explain *"no difference in the LEDs"* on **every Setup-only card**,
and LUMEN is the most Setup-only card in the catalog: `timer: ""`, nothing runs
after the field is painted.

**It is dismissed because the rest of the same bench sheet is a list of
observations of cards visibly doing things after install** - ARC's centre press,
MORPH's corner taps, STAGE's zones, POMODORO's intervals. If a written Setup
never ran, the bench sheet would be one line long. **No bench cycle was spent on
it and none should be.** `docs/INSTALL-RUNBOOK.md` is where a real answer would
live if one is ever wanted.

### Why there is no re-cut, which is the finding underneath the branch

`8*max(@DEPTH)` has to stay inside the subtrahend **36** or the bottom row's `d`
goes negative and a colour channel **truncates rather than clamping** - the
lower-bound half of the entry's own four-corner proof, **cited rather than
re-derived**, which shows the obvious alternative form going negative at
`@DEPTH 6` and exactly black at 8.

**So 4 is the largest value the arithmetic admits, and `{1, 2, 3, 4}` is
therefore the ONLY four-element set of positive integers this knob can carry.**
The ramp is already at full travel. **The plan's re-cut branch asks for an
instrument that does not exist**, and re-cutting inside the set could only make
the knob shallower.

**This is reported as a SHORTFALL with alternatives, never as a trimmed version
of the ask** - success criterion 9. Both routes are costed in the entry header:

| Route | Character cost | What it costs elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| **move the default**, index 2 -> 3 | **zero**, no arithmetic change | rewrites eighty of eighty-one cells in `frames.json`, moves the OG image, and spends the knob's last step so the card ships with no room to go deeper |
| **subtrahend and divisor both 32**, `d = 32 - row*@DEPTH` with `anchor*d//32`, the same four values | **608 at the picker corner - MEASURED, and it is the same 608 the entry costs today** | the bottom row reaches **exact black** at `@DEPTH 4` (`0,0,0`, frame falling from **171** non-zero bytes to **152**); at the shipped default the bottom row becomes **62,21,0** where it is **84,29,0** today, so `frames.json` and the OG image move; and the never-black guarantee has to be restated as `8*max(@DEPTH) <= 32` with equality allowed |

**Neither is shipped, and that is a decision rather than an omission.** Both
change the picture the card is known by; the note being answered described an
effect the simulator says is already present; and **only the user's bench can
say which, if either, is the change they wanted.**

**A phase that shipped a ramp re-cut it could not justify, to have something in
the diff, is the failure this phase's gates exist to catch.** The table is the
evidence and the shortfall is the deliverable.
