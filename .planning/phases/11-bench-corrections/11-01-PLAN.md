**The two idioms being rescued, verbatim, before their files are deleted.**

*The literal-parameterised form*, from `life.ts:59-61`:

> THE DECAY LENGTH DIVIDES 252. The rate is `256 - 252//28 = 247` and the starting phase is 252, so
> the phase steps by 9 and lands on exactly 0 in exactly 28 ticks. A length that does not divide 252
> expires part-way down.

with the correction the research measured and `life.ts` does not state: the rule is stricter than
"divides 252" — **the timeout must be an exact divisor of 252**, because the emitted step is `252//T`
and the walk lands on `252 - T*(252//T)`. The fifteen legal timeouts under 256 are
**4, 6, 7, 9, 12, 14, 18, 21, 28, 36, 42, 63, 84, 126, 252**. 252 is the right start because
`252 = 2^2 x 3^2 x 7` has eighteen divisors against 255's eight, and 256 cannot be a start because the
field is a byte.

*The computed form*, from `gridlock.ts:129` and its `@SPREAD` comment at `:194-200`:

```
local p=glim(248-math.max(math.abs(n%9-u),math.abs(n//9-v))*@SPREAD,0,248)
glpfs(a,1,p,4,0) glt(a,1,(256-p)//4)
```

> EVERY VALUE IS A MULTIPLE OF FOUR, because the derived timeout is `(256 - p)//4` and it has to be
> exact. A value that is not would strand a ring part-way down with no Timer to repaint it.

**`cull.ts` survives and is the phase's one shipped worked example of the literal form**
(`glpfs(a,1,252,256-252//@FLASH,0)` + `glt(a,1,@FLASH)`, values `["12","28","42","63"]`, **all four
divisors of 252** — measured, and something `11-RESEARCH.md` did not check). The gate's header cites
it rather than restating it.

**The mechanism.** `pha` is a `uint8_t` and `+=` **wraps** — `grid_led.c:190-211`, reproduced line for
line at `pad-sim.ts:883-891` as `L.pha = (L.pha + L.fre) & 255`. Nothing clamps and nothing stops. The
freeze point is `(start + rate x timeout) mod 256` and shape 0 renders intensity = phase. The
ROADMAP's "the phase walks down to 3 and stops" is **wrong in mechanism and optimistic in
consequence** and this plan's SUMMARY says so.
