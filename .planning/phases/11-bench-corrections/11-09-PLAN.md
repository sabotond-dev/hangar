## ARC — the amplitude, +21 of 527 free

`arc.ts:50` paints the heart with the raw triangle and emits the depth-scaled value:

```lua
-- painted:  glp(a,1,v)                         -- raw
-- emitted:  glim(64+(v-128)*s.d//255,0,127)    -- depth-scaled by s.d = 127 - y
```

At `y = 127` the depth is 0, the CC pins at 64, and the heart keeps pulsing at full scale. Scale the
heart by the same `s.d` and the picture becomes the output. **251 → 272 in the Timer, +21, 636 free.**

**Read `arc.ts`'s header first.** It carries a capitalised note about its keeper timeout
(`glt(a,2,65535)` beside a zero-rate `glpfs`), which is legitimate and must not be "fixed" —
`lua-smoke.spec.ts` test 3 guards the strobe case and this is the other one.

## The four questions, and the evidence for each

**Ask them together, once.** Four separate pauses across four plans would be four interruptions for
one conversation.

### ARC — "if you press the center it stops, pressing it again resumes"

- **Reading 1: "stops" means the output went flat.** At the bottom edge `s.d = 0`, the CC pins at 64,
  and the card is dead while the heart still moves. Task 01 fixes exactly that. Consistent with
  *"pressing it again resumes"* if the second press was higher up the pad.
- **Reading 2: something on hardware the simulator does not reproduce.** ARC has no stop, no resume
  and no toggle in its source, and its oscillator cannot reach rate 0.

**Either way this is also a bench row**, because reading 2 can only be settled at the module.

### STAGE — "Lining up breathing is missing"

- **Reading 1: a third zone state.** Live and held exist; a **preview / standby** zone — the scene you
  are *about to* cut to — has no indication at all. **The shipped description already promises it**:
  *"the live one glows and the one you are lining up breathes"* (`listing.ts:371`). Real feature work,
  399 characters free.
- **Reading 2: the four corners breathe out of step.** Read against the source they do not — `Z(z,f)`
  arms `glpfs(a,1,0,f,3)` on all four corners **from phase 0 in the same call**. If the user saw them
  drift, that is a hardware finding.

**Put reading 1's evidence in front of the user**, quoting their own card's description. Do not
present the two as equally weighted when the shipped copy already takes a side — but do not decide.

### LUMEN — "the color depth / opacity doesn't work"

Asked here so 11-10 is not blocked on a second pause.

- **Reading 1: the ramp is too shallow.** `d = 36 - row*@DEPTH` runs 36..4, so the bottom row is 78 %
  of the anchor at DEPTH 1 and 11 % at DEPTH 4, and **never black**. Turning the knob at the low
  settings changes very little. Work: re-cut `@DEPTH`, possibly to reach black.
- **Reading 2: opacity should be an emitted value.** LUMEN sends only raw X and Y on `@CC` and
  `@CC+1`; a lighting desk receives a **position**, not a colour and not an intensity. Work: a third
  stream — or the hex-over-sysex change 11-10 makes it moot.

### MORPH — "mapping mode needed in"

MORPH's note has three clauses. The stuck colours are 11-02's and the suppression is 11-08's; **this
clause is the one nobody can pin down from the words**, and guessing it would spend a stamp slot on a
feature that may not be the one asked for.

- **Reading 1: an assignment mode, for the receiving software.** MORPH emits **all four** corner CCs
  on the same sample, so a DAW's MIDI-learn binds whichever message it happened to catch and the other
  three are unbindable without hand-editing. A "mapping mode" would emit **one corner at a time**,
  under the user's control, so each can be learned in turn. **Cost shape:** a mode latch, a selection
  gesture and a single-corner emit branch. The latch and the branch are small; **the gesture is the
  expensive and undecided part**, exactly as it is for STAGE's third state on the same nine-cell pad
  with no modifier. Cost at least two gestures before shipping this.
- **Reading 2: a mapping *curve*, as a knob.** The four weights are a bilinear corner split
  (`u*v//127` and its three siblings). "Mapping mode" could mean a choice of how position becomes
  weight — bilinear against nearest-corner/exclusive, or a steeper response. **Cost shape:** one
  **appended** knob (never inserted — a knob position is stamp payload) plus an alternate weight
  expression at one site. Materially cheaper than reading 1, and it is a knob-array change with the
  usual SHARE consequence.
- **Reading 3: nothing to build.** "Mapping mode" may describe what the user does in their host rather
  than something the card lacks, in which case the answer is a documentation line and not characters.

**MORPH's budget is not the constraint on any of the three** — 400 free at worst before this phase,
less 11-02's +8 and whatever the `@DECAY` re-cut moved, less 11-08's suppression guard. **Carry the
figure 11-08 left rather than the one above**, and cost the chosen reading against that.

### The gesture is the design problem, and there are THREE to cost — one of them free

`stage.ts:141`'s `Z(z,f)` takes a breathe **RATE, not a flag**: `f = 0` paints the idle box, `f > 0`
paints a live box in `@LIVEC` on layer 2 and arms a breathe on layer 1 at rate `f`. **A third state is
a third rate, and that part is nearly free.** The expensive part is **selecting** the preview zone, and
the pad is already spoken for: `e==4 or e>8` cuts to a zone and fires the keystroke, `e>=5` returns the
last zone to live.

**Cost at least two of these three. State the chosen one and the rejected ones in the entry header.**

- **(a) The card ALREADY distinguishes a tap from a hold, and it costs nothing.**
  `Z(z, e>8 and 4 or 24)` — a coalesced DOWNUP gets rate 4, a held press gets 24. **That
  discrimination is already computed**, so mapping one of the two to "line up" and the other to "cut"
  is a re-reading of an existing branch rather than a new gesture. **The price is real and must be
  stated as a decision rather than arrive as a side effect: today BOTH cut.** This is a behaviour
  reversal on a card an operator may already have installed, not an addition.
- **(b) A second contact.** STAGE is **the one entry in the catalog whose `touch_cb` does not reject
  `i > 0`** — every other card returns immediately for a second finger, and STAGE currently treats one
  as another cut. So a second finger is free to mean "line up". **Verify FIRST that `i > 0` is
  deliverable at all through the Lua host and the shipped `TouchSampler`.** If the simulator can never
  deliver it, the preview cannot show the feature and this option is out — a card whose headline
  gesture is invisible in the browser is the failure `PREV-01` exists to prevent. **Measure it; do not
  assume it in either direction.**
- **(c) A long press.** STAGE has a Timer (2560 ms) that could count, which most of the catalog does
  not. The most conventional answer and the most expensive: a per-contact counter, a threshold and a
  cancel path, against 390 free.

**A FIFTH KNOB IS NOT AN OPTION; IT IS A STOP.** `stamp.ts:324`'s `shapeOf` sums a rack's option
counts, so adding a knob — a preview colour, say — moves STAGE's shape character and demotes every
STAGE link ever shared from `restored` to `older`. **11-09's own task 01 spent that tripwire on
POMODORO for two intervals the user asked for by name; it is not spent again for a colour nobody asked
for.** The third state's colour comes out of the existing `@LIVEC`/`@ZONEC` arithmetic. If the executor
concludes a knob is required, that is a **finding and a stop**, with the shape move named and the
knob-free alternative proposed alongside.

### What does NOT move

- **`listing.ts:371` most likely does not change**, because it already describes exactly what ships.
  **Say so in a line** rather than leaving its absence from the diff unexplained. It moves only if the
  shipped gesture makes the existing sentence inaccurate in a **new** way.
- **The keeper is 30000 on a single-digit-rate layer and it is LEGITIMATE** — STAGE's header says so in
  capitals, and the reason is that the guard discriminates on rate. A third rate stays in the same
  order of magnitude or the guard's discrimination changes. `decay-idiom.spec.ts` checks it.
- **The Timer refreshes `glt` and does NOT re-issue `glpfs`**, and the header explains why in capitals:
  a re-issue writes phase 0 and snaps a breathe back to black mid-breath at an arbitrary moment. **A
  third state needing its own timeout refresh extends the Timer's existing loop; it does not add a
  `glpfs`.**
- **`touch-guard.spec.ts`'s one `DECLARED_EXCEPTIONS` row is STAGE's**, keyed on the whole branch
  clause `if e==4 or e>8 then … elseif e>=5 then`. A new branch **must not invalidate the reason that
  row states**, and `DECLARED_EXCEPTIONS.length` stays **2** with no new row.
- **`frames.json` is expected not to move and the expectation is PROVED, not asserted.** The fixture
  runs each entry at its defaults with **no gesture**, and a preview zone exists only once a finger has
