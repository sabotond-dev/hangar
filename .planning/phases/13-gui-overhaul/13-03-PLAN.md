<objective>
The largest single visual change in the phase, and it is not the layout.

HANGAR renders **every sentence in acid lime at an alpha** - a three-rung ladder of `--color-ink`
0.72, `--color-ink-quiet` 0.55 and `--color-ink-dim` 0.5 over true black, nine tokens in all, gated by
`identity.spec.ts` so hard that a **tenth `--color-*` is red and a fourth hue anywhere in the file is
red**. The Bible renders every sentence in **`#F0F1E9`**, every secondary line in **`#ACB3A2`**, over
**four warm graphite surfaces**, and reserves the lime for the primary action and the current
selection. All five PDF pages confirm it.

**So `identity.spec.ts` is not amended by this plan. It is rewritten**, and its central sentence
changes from *"the only third hue is the over-budget alarm"* to *"these eleven values and no twelfth"*.
That is the honest description of what happened: the count moved from nine to eleven, and the **idea**
moved from "one hue at three alphas" to "graphite surfaces, near-white text, one action colour".

**Two things the research computed that must survive as rules, not as prose.**

1. **The divider fails 3:1 on every surface** - 1.71, 1.56, 1.39 - and that is acceptable **only while
   it stays decorative**, which is what WCAG 1.4.11 exempts and what §12 calls it. But the PDF uses
   1px rules for table row separators, panel edges and the whole inspector's boundary, and §3 asks to
   *"replace strong borders around every subcontrol with restrained section dividers"*. **The rule
   this plan ships and gates: anything that bounds a control or communicates a boundary uses
   `--color-boundary` (4.57 / 4.18 / 3.73 - passes on all three surfaces); `--color-divider` is used
   only where removing it would change nothing but taste.** That rule is exactly the sort that erodes,
   so it is a test.
2. **Selection cannot be a fill.** Raised-on-panel is **1.12:1** and panel-on-workspace is **1.09:1**;
   a selected row distinguished by its background alone is invisible. §14 says *"never make selection
   depend only on color"* and **the PDF already solves it**: every selected rail row carries a **3px
   action-colour left rule** (16.65:1 against the workspace) **plus** the raised fill **plus** an
   action-coloured label. Three signals. The gate holds all three, so that a later plan cannot drop
   the rule as decoration.

**Typography stays Grifter + Inter (D-04)** and the research's honest observation is kept rather than
smoothed: **Grifter is a bold-only static face at 700 in this tree**, and at the intro's 62-66px it
reads heavier and narrower than the PDF's face, which sits around 500-600. That is a difference, not a
fault, and the SUMMARY records it beside the rendered result rather than claiming a match. Grifter
keeps the headlines and the 11px uppercase micro labels - which is what it is good at - and Inter
keeps every sentence. **Grifter's OpenType name table declares PERSONAL USE**; the swap token exists so
the fix is two lines, and this plan neither resolves nor hides that.

**The wordmark stops being type.** `hangar-logo-w.svg` - 6 paths, all `#ffffff`, ink 698.6 x 86.7
inside an 810 x 810 viewBox that is 78% empty - replaces the wide-tracked Grifter text wordmark, which
also takes Grifter out of the one place its licence is most visible. Per D-14 Q14 it is re-cropped and
its fills become `currentColor`, **and the original stays byte-identical in `bible/`**, proved by
hash rather than by intention.

Purpose: the palette, the type and the mark the next sixteen waves are built out of, gated so none of
them can drift.
Output: `app.css` rewritten at its `@theme` block and its pill removed, `identity.spec.ts` rewritten to
eleven tests, one new component, one new asset, one allowlist row cleared. Term `+0 / +5`.
</objective>
