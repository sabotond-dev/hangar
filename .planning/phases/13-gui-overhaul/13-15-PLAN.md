**What the runtime is.** From the research's measured design, at **861 characters** before D-08's
rotary:

- refuses non-live codes with the blessed spelling `e~=1 and e~=4 and e<9` and hands them to the
  library's expiry so the contact's release runs;
- computes the cell as `y*9//128*9 + x*9//128` and reads the region **straight out of `M`** - no search
  loop, because 13-14 precomputed the 81-entry map;
- **a contact keeps the region it landed in** (`S[i]`) for the whole gesture, until a lift or an expiry.
  This is better than hysteresis and cheaper: you do not want a finger dragged off the end of a fader
  to start driving the XY pad beside it. **Phase 12's `Q` and `W` are therefore not on the Sandbox's hot
  path**; they stay for the hand-authored entries, and the SUMMARY says so rather than letting a reader
  assume the Sandbox uses them;
- calls the library's finger light on layer 2, above the region's own paint on layer 1 - the probe's
  finding 5 applies to a Sandbox surface for exactly the same reason;
- **defines `R`** - Phase 12's release convention - so a Button's note-off fires on **every** expiry
  path, including the lost lift the probe measured **four times out of five**. That is not polish; it is
  the difference between a toy and something a musician will put in front of an audience.

**The Knob (D-08), and the arithmetic that sets its minimum size.** The user chose a **real rotary
gesture** over the free vertical-drag-with-a-round-graphic the research recommended: angle around the
region's centre with a wrap-safe accumulator, `math.atan` available because `grid_lua.c:523` opens
`LUA_MATHLIBNAME`. D-08 costs it at **+150-200 characters plus a per-contact previous-angle table** and
notes that *"on a 3x3 region a full turn is nine cells of travel, so the minimum sensible Knob region is
a question for the compiler"*.

**That question has an arithmetic answer and this plan derives it rather than guessing.** Touch is
continuous 0..127 per axis, not quantised to cells - Probe A Q5 reached 0/1 and 126/127, so the pad
reaches its edges. A 3x3 region spans about **42 raw units** per axis, so a finger at its edge is ~21
units from the centre and `atan(dy, dx)` is perfectly stable there. **What is unstable is the centre**:
within a few units of it, a one-unit jitter swings the angle wildly. So the real constraint is a
**centre dead zone**, not a cell count, and the "nine cells of travel" figure is about the **LED
feedback** - how many lights a full turn walks past - and not about resolution. **Derive the dead
zone's radius from the jitter Probe A Q1 measured**, state it in raw units, and say which of the two
readings each number belongs to.

**And it earns a bench row**, because nothing about a rotary gesture on this pad has ever been felt.
The row is written here and handed over at 13-20.

## The seven tests, every one in a real VM

Driven through `createLuaHost` exactly as `lua-smoke.spec.ts` drives a gesture - **read `:100-140` and
`:429-470` and reuse the mechanism**.

1. **A contact keeps its region.** Press inside a fader, drag off its edge into the neighbouring XY
   pad's cells, and assert **the fader keeps receiving the value** and the XY pad receives nothing -
   until a lift. Then press again inside the XY pad and assert it responds. This is `S[i]`, and it is
   the Sandbox's central behaviour.
2. **Every release path fires `R`.** Four paths, exactly as 12-07 proved for the library: an end code; a
   **same-id re-press** without a lift (the probe's Q6.5 case, which firmware makes the *normal* case
   because it assigns the lowest free id); a press on a cell held by another contact; and the Timer
   sweep after the caller's window. **A Button's note-off is asserted on all four**, not just the first.
3. **A vertical fader's value** is `127-(y-y0)*127//(y1-y0)` at the region's own bounds, sent on change
   only: assert the top and bottom rows give 127 and 0 exactly, that a still finger sends nothing, and
   that the precomputed `y0`/`y1` are used rather than re-derived.
4. **The XY pad** sends per-axis on change, scaled to the region's bounds, on two CCs - which is Phase
   12's `A` with the region's bounds instead of the pad's. **Assert one CC per moved axis**, which is
   the probe's rule 3 and the thing LUMEN was corrected for.
5. **The Knob**: a full clockwise turn accumulates monotonically through the 359°->1° wrap **without a
   -357 step**; a counter-clockwise turn decrements; the value clamps at both ends; **and a finger
   inside the centre dead zone changes nothing.**
6. **The class gates over the runtime string**, using `touch-guard.spec.ts`'s and
   `decay-idiom.spec.ts`'s **own needles**, not re-written ones.
7. **The measured cost**: canonical under `compressScript` after `padReady()`, printed **beside the
   research's 861** and beside **D-08's 150-200 estimate for the rotary's share**, with the difference
   named. If the total does not fit its slot, that is a **finding**, and the fallback order below says
   what goes.

## The fallback order, if the runtime does not fit

**Never** the region keeping (`S[i]`), the release convention (`R`) or the expiry sweep - between them
they are the probe's rules 2 and the difference between a toy and an instrument.

1. **The Knob's rotary** goes back to the free vertical-drag branch (the research's option (a)), which
   costs zero because it *is* the fader branch. **This reverses D-08 and is therefore not the executor's
   call**: it is recorded as a finding with the measured overage, and the user decides at 13-20. **It is
   also one of the two prices the re-ask above names**, and 13-02's own `two-slots` option lists it - so
   if the measurement forces this step, the user has already been shown what it costs.
2. **The horizontal fader** merges into the vertical branch with one `t==2` ternary (about +30 rather
   than a second branch).
3. **The XY pad** is deferred with the characters it would have cost written down. **The second of the
   two prices in the re-ask**, and also listed on 13-02's `two-slots` option.

**A request that cannot fit is a finding, not a failure**, and it is recorded with the number.

## The Knob's arithmetic, to be derived and stated

| Quantity | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| A region's span in raw units | `(w * 128) / 9` per axis - a 3x3 is about 42 |
| The angular resolution at the edge | `atan` of continuous values; **not** cell-quantised |
| The unstable zone | within a few raw units of the centre, where one unit of jitter swings the angle |
| The jitter's size | **Probe A Q1** measured a still finger moving every 10 ms sample - use that figure |
| The dead-zone radius | derived from the two above, stated in raw units and in cells |
| "Nine cells of travel per turn" | **the LED feedback**, not the resolution - say which reading each number belongs to |

**The minimum region size follows from the dead zone**: a region whose half-span is not comfortably
larger than the dead-zone radius has no usable annulus. State the rule as an inequality and the
resulting minimum in cells, and **ledger the refusal message**.
