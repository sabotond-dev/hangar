<objective>
The rest of the words, and the twenty-seven names.

**`ARC` becomes `Arc`.** Every entry name in the catalog is uppercase - `ARC`, `LUMEN`, `EUCLID`,
`CHORUS` - and D-05 confines uppercase to *"short section labels and breadcrumbs… never for sentences,
headlines or instructions"*. The PDF shows `Arc`, `Aurora`, `Chorus` in exactly the place HANGAR shows
`ARC`. D-14 Q11b already decided it: **yes, and it is the most visible single application of D-05.**

**A name is not an id, and this plan proves it rather than assuming it.** The ids are lowercase slugs
(`arc`, `lumen`), and they are what `frames.json` is keyed by, what `static/og/<id>.png` is named after,
what `/c/<id>` or `/playground/<id>` addresses, and what the stamp's payload validates against.
**Nothing about a link moves.** What moves: the rendered name everywhere, `ogAlt`'s text (so every OG
image is re-rendered), any test literal asserting a name, and `browse/typographic.ts` if it cases
anything.

**Fifty-six exports in `tune/copy.ts`**, and most of them are HANGAR mechanics the Bible never saw. Each
keeps its **fact** and changes its **words**:

| Family | The fact it carries | The Bible's line |
|---|---|---|
| the two meter families | how many characters of 908 are used, and the forecast for a knob at its longest | **none** - HANGAR's own honesty device |
| the ladder | a feature was trimmed to stay in budget, and which | **none** (`TUNE-04`'s own requirement) |
| the eleven picker strings | the RGB444 lattice, the cheap steps, the picker corner | **none** |
| `HOLD` / `HELD` | a knob is locked against Randomize | §7 permits locks; **`Lock` / `Locked` reads better in sentence case** |
| `SURPRISE ME` -> `Randomize`, `RESET ALL` -> `Reset settings`, `COPY LINK` -> `Share snapshot` | unchanged behaviour | §7 and the PDF - **verbatim** |
| the three stamp landings | restored, older, unreadable | **none**, and `SHARE-03`'s "made with an older version" is the fact that must survive |
| the eight live-region announcements | what changed, once, without announcing every frame | §14 |
| `ogAlt` | what the picture shows | **none** |
| **`MIX TWO`, `THIS ONE`, `THAT ONE`, `TURN IT DOWN`** | nothing - the feature is gone | **removed here by name**, left standing by 13-10 so its term stayed honest |

**Three requirement rows are touched by the labels and each is 13-20's to amend**: `TUNE-07` names
`SURPRISE ME`, `SHARE-02` names `COPY LINK`, `TUNE-06` names the double-click reset. This plan changes
the strings and **names the rows for 13-20**; it does not edit `REQUIREMENTS.md`.

**This is the one wave that touches every entry file**, so it is the one wave that runs the sweep. The
sweep re-walks every entry's knob space at the picker corner; a re-cased name cannot move a shape
character - **names are not knobs** - but the sweep is the only thing that would notice if something
else moved with them, and *"it cannot have"* is not a measurement.

Purpose: the last of the words, and the twenty-seven names the whole register change is most visible in.

`+0 / +0`.

## The rename, mechanically

1. **Find every name**: `src/lib/catalog/entries/*.ts` (`name:`), `presets.ts` (the nine), and any
   fixture that carries one.
2. **Title case, the Bible's way**: `ARC` -> `Arc`, `NINE PADS` -> `Nine pads`, `RADAR POINTS` ->
   `Radar points`. **Sentence case for multi-word names, not Title Case Of Every Word** - D-05's own
   rule is sentence case, and the PDF's `Notes & chords` is the evidence. **`ZONA` stays uppercase**
   wherever it appears in a name or a line: it is a product name.
3. **Grep for every literal** that asserts a name: `catalog/copy.spec.ts`, `listing.spec.ts`,
   `frames.spec.ts`, `audition.spec.ts`, `stamp.spec.ts`, `front-door.spec.ts`, the e2e files, and
   `docs/HARDWARE-AUDITION.md`'s rows. **Move them all in one commit**; a rename split across two
   commits is a red suite in the middle.
4. **`ogAlt` and `static/og/`**: the alt text carries the name, so **every image is re-rendered**.
   Filenames are ids and do not move - **prove it**: the file list before and after must be identical,
   and only the bytes change.
5. **`frames.json`** is keyed by id and must **not** move. `git diff --quiet` it, and if it moved,
   something is keyed by name that should not be.

**Say in the SUMMARY how many literals moved, in how many files.** That number is the honest size of a
decision that reads like a one-line change.

## `tune/copy.ts`, family by family

Every export keeps its fact and changes its words per the objective's table. Three specific moves:

- **`HOLD`/`HELD` become `Lock`/`Locked`.** §7 permits parameter locks *"only when the configuration has
  enough randomizable parameters to justify them"*; they exist and are justified; and `SURPRISE_ALL_HELD`
  - the degenerate case where every knob is locked - keeps its behaviour and gets a sentence-case line.
- **The three stamp landings** keep `SHARE-03`'s fact - a link made with an older version lands on the
  base configuration and says so - in the new register. **The fact is a requirement; the words are not.**
- **`MIX TWO`'s four strings are deleted by name**, closing the row 13-10 opened.

## What `catalog/copy.spec.ts` and `listing.spec.ts` still require

`listing.spec.ts` **requires a `quiet` line for every entry whose motion is not self-evident**. D-14
Q11c put `description` on the card and `quiet` in the workspace as helper text - **both keep their
roles**, and this plan does not merge them. The specs' counts hold; their literals move.

`browse/typographic.ts` is read: if it upper-cases or letter-spaces a name anywhere, that is now a
**rendering** decision that contradicts D-05, and it is removed with a sentence rather than left to
fight the data.
