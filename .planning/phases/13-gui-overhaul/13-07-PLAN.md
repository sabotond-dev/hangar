<objective>
The first screen, and the end of the coverflow's ground.

**What the PDF's page 1 is.** No nav, no context bar, no rail, no inspector. The header carries only
the wordmark, the words `Quick guide`, and an outlined `Connect ZONA`. Two columns:

**Left** - an 11px tracked eyebrow `WELCOME TO YOUR CONFIGURATION STUDIO`; a two-line headline at
62-66px with **`Make ZONA`** in near-white and **`your own.`** in the action colour; two 19px secondary
lines; **two stacked start cards** (`START WITH AN IDEA` / **Explore Playground** / *"Discover
configurations. Try one. Make it yours."* with a **3px action-colour left rule** and a 48x48 filled
action square carrying an arrow; then `START WITH A BLANK SURFACE` / **Build in Sandbox** / *"Arrange
controls and choose what each gesture does."* with a 1px boundary and a 48x48 outlined square carrying
a plus); a quiet line `Already have a configuration?` beside a link `Import config`; and one bulleted
line *"Start in browser preview. Connect ZONA when you're ready."*

**Right** - a panel with `TRY THE SURFACE` and a small filled `BROWSER PREVIEW` chip, containing a
**live 9 x 9 matrix** running ARC, with `ARC / MODULATION` and *"Drag across the surface to preview"*
beneath it.

**Bottom** - a full-width rule and three numbered items: `01 Explore`, `02 Shape`, `03 Apply`.

**§3 would have permitted texture here and the PDF declines it.** *"Use textured imagery on an optional
introduction screen"* is the one clause that could have saved the glyph field, and the PDF's own intro
is flat and solid with a live surface where the texture would be. D-09 already decided it; this plan is
where `Splash.svelte` and `glyph-field.ts` actually leave, because this is where `/` stops rendering
them.

**The returning visitor, and why there is no redirect** (D-14 Q2, and the research measured all three
reasons in this tree): `+layout.ts` sets `prerender = true`, so a client-side redirect **flashes the
intro before it jumps**; `/` is the OG target for every unfurl and a redirect breaks the `<head>` a
Discord card reads; and the live surface is the site's single best first impression. **So `/` is always
the intro, and the flag changes what Card A offers** - `Resume draft`, pointing at the newest draft,
for a visitor who has one. §4's *"never interrupt returning users"* is satisfied without a flash,
without losing the OG page, and without a navigation on mount.

**A storage that throws sees the intro every time.** That is the safe direction and it is stated as a
decision, because the alternative - assuming "seen" on a read failure - hides the site's front door
from the people whose browsers are strictest.

**What happens to the front door's three properties.** `front-door.ts:150-176` asserts that the
declared motion equals the golden-frames derivation, that `restsBlack` agrees with it, and that **no
two quiet pads are adjacent on the ring**. The PDF has no ring - it has **one** surface. So the
adjacency property **loses its subject and is deleted by name**; the other two survive, re-aimed at the
single hero, because *"the hero must not be a dark pad"* is exactly the same rule with one member.
**Note for the record**: if Phase 12's 12-06 answered `trail`, that plan re-derived the seven-entry
ring - work this deletes. 13-VALIDATION records it as D-7 and 13-20 carries it to the user.

Purpose: the first thing anyone sees, and the last time this repository maintains a dissolve.
Output: one route rewritten, three components, four tests, two files deleted, five unit titles and
seven e2e titles removed by name, one added. Term **`+0 / -1`**, e2e **`-6 / -6`**.
