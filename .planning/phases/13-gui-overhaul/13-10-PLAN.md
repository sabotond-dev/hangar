<objective>
The workspace's second half: the honesty devices re-homed, the randomiser given a scope, the monitor
built from a log that already exists, and MIX TWO cut.

**The five mechanics the Bible never saw** (research Q8): the 908 budget meters and their forecast, the
fit ladder's back-off, the RGB444 picker's cheap-step marks, the device disclosure, and MIX TWO. Four of
them survive and are re-homed. **The meters are the one the site cannot lose**: the spec has no budget
meter anywhere because the spec's author never had to fit a configuration into 908 characters, and
*"you have thirty-three characters left"* is precisely what makes a visitor trust the install button.
They land **in the inspector, under the MIDI section**, where the PDF has room and where the numbers
sit next to the fields that move them. The ladder's message goes with them.

**MIX TWO is cut (D-12).** Built in Phase 10, property-tested, **never mounted** - `grep` shows
`MixTwo.svelte` imported by nothing but `FrontDoor`, which 13-09 deleted. Its `mix.ts` index-vector
crossover and its two property tests go with it, and **the phase records the deletion by name so
nothing later mistakes it for a gap.** That is D-12's own sentence.

**Randomize gains a scope, and that is a behaviour change.** §7: *"Default scope: appearance and
compatible expressive behavior. Preserve MIDI destination, channel, routing, and device target."*
Today `surpriseIndices` rolls **every unheld knob**, and several entries carry a `@CH` channel knob with
sixteen options and a `@CC` controller knob. Under the spec those are excluded from the default roll.
It is a small change to one function and a real change to what the button does, and it is the right
change: rolling somebody's MIDI channel mid-session is the one surprise nobody wants.
`SURPRISE_ROLL_LIMIT = 12` and `SURPRISE_BUDGET_MS = 400` do not move.

**Undo randomize is new and is one value.** `surpriseIndices` returns the **previous** index vector;
the button restores it. No history stack, no undo tree. **It is explicitly not general undo** - §17's
Sandbox undo/redo is a different thing with a different owner (13-16), and conflating them is how a
one-value control becomes a subsystem.

**The monitor is mostly built already.** `src/lib/sim/lua-host.ts:295` keeps
`private readonly midiLog: HostMidi[]`, appended at `:697` by the bridged `__hangar_gms`, plus a
separate sysex log. §10's six columns - timestamp, direction, source, channel, message, value - are a
render of that array plus a clock, with `direction` constant `out` and `source` constant
`Browser preview` in v1. **Three honest limits, and D-14 Q4b already decided the first:**

1. **The nine compiler-driven entries produce nothing** - the vendored `pad-sim.ts` has no MIDI log at
   all. So the monitor appears on **Lua entries only** and the bar is **absent**, not present-and-empty,
   on the others. Adding a log to `src/vendor/` would be a declared divergence for a v1 nicety.
2. **Rate.** Probe A Q1 measured a *still* finger emitting a message every 10 ms sample. §10 says
   aggregate or limit. **Coalesce by `(channel, cmd, p1)` inside a 100 ms window with an `xN` count, and
   cap the visible log at 200 rows with a ring.**
3. **`No MIDI output` is the truth and stays the truth.** §19's Web MIDI row was closed by Phase 6:
   HANGAR never uses Web MIDI for configuration. The PDF's own phrasing draws the line and it is kept
   verbatim.

Purpose: the honest numbers survive the redesign, the randomiser stops touching the wire, and the log
that already exists becomes visible.
Output: two components re-homed, one created, one function scoped, three files deleted, four titles
removed and three added. Term **`-1 / -1`**.

**`PREV-04` is not on this plan any more.** It sat in the frontmatter with no task behind it while
13-09 - which actually builds the `Configure` / `▷ Play` switch and says in its own words *"do not tick
it"* - carried no row at all. **13-09 carries `PREV-04` now**, 13-16 carries it for the Sandbox's
Edit/Play, and 13-20 decides whether the set closes it. 13-VALIDATION's D-11 and its task map are
corrected to match.

## Randomize's scope, precisely

```
roll:     every unheld knob whose kind is NOT a MIDI destination
exclude:  any knob whose id or kind names a MIDI channel or controller
```

**Derive the exclusion from the knob descriptors, not from a hard-coded id list** - `knobs.lua.ts` and
`knobs.preset.ts` both produce `KnobDescriptor { id, label, kind, options, default }`, and the exclusion
is a predicate over that. **List every knob the predicate excludes, by entry and id**, in the SUMMARY:
that list is what the change actually did, and if it is empty the change is theatre and the plan says
so.

`SURPRISE_ROLL_LIMIT = 12`, `SURPRISE_BUDGET_MS = 400` and `SURPRISE_ALL_HELD`'s degenerate case do not
move. **If excluding the MIDI knobs makes an entry's roll degenerate** - every remaining knob held -
`SURPRISE_ALL_HELD` already covers it, and the SUMMARY names any entry where that became reachable.
