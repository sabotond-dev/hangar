<objective>
The first deletion, and the first negative term in this project's history.

**What goes, and why it is one plan rather than six.** The CRT scanline layers, the halftone grain, the
dot-field/lattice ground and the `SCREEN` switch are **global**: they live in `app.css`, in
`PadFrame`/`PadCanvas`, and in `+layout.svelte`'s footer. They are not any route's composition, so
they can be removed before a single route is rewritten - which is why the research wanted the deletion
early and why this half of it can be. §3 is explicit: *"use solid surfaces rather than background
photography inside the working application"*, and the PDF's own intro - the one screen §3 would have
allowed texture on - is **flat, solid, with a live surface where the texture would be**. D-09 removes
all of it.

**What does NOT go here.** `FrontDoor`, `Coverflow`, `NamePlate`, `Splash`, `PadSpinner` and
`ChosenPanel` are a **composition**, and `FrontDoor.svelte` is imported by both `src/routes/+page.svelte`
and `src/routes/c/[id]/+page.svelte`. Deleting them now would break two routes and every e2e title that
walks them for five waves. They die at 13-07 and 13-09, as each route stops rendering them. The
research put the whole deletion at wave 3; **this is the correction, and 13-VALIDATION records it as
D-5.**

**The count goes down.** `-9` on tests, `-4 / -8` on e2e. 13-01 proved the counting gate handles a
negative delta and named the two real exposures; this is the plan that spends the proof. **Every
deleted title is named below, re-read from the file before deletion, and named again in the SUMMARY.**
A title in the file that is not on the list stops the deletion and is reported - the list is the
planner's reading of `d78e087` and the tree has had a phase land since.

**The one thing that must not be lost with the texture.** §14 asks to *"keep motion generated by
instrument output controllable"*, and the `SCREEN` switch was HANGAR's answer: a visible, non-OS
control that stops the moving layers. The layers are going; **the purpose is not.** It re-homes under
`Help & shortcuts` - the footer link the PDF draws on all four shell pages - as a plain motion
preference that stops the ambient previews, sitting beside `prefers-reduced-motion` rather than
overriding it. This plan lands the **control's home and its behaviour**; 13-05 builds the footer that
carries the link, so the control lands as a self-contained component the shell mounts.

**Phase 4's reduced-motion contract is not touched.** `src/lib/sim/host.ts:267` reads
`matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)")` and `:733-739` snaps a normal entry to **tick 64**
rather than tick 0, because tick 0 is a pad two-thirds unlit. That is correct, §6 and §14 ask for
exactly it, and this plan reads it and changes nothing. What moves is only **where the browser-side
proof of it lives**, because `aesthetic.e2e.ts` is deleted - which the research left as an open Wave 0
question and which is answered here: **beside the surviving untagged "reduced motion stills every card"
in `browse.e2e.ts`**, because that is where the surviving subject lives.

Purpose: the phase stops maintaining a look it is replacing, and proves it can subtract.
Output: four sources edited, one component deleted, one e2e file deleted, nine unit titles and five
e2e titles removed by name, one re-homed. Term **`+0 / -9`**, e2e **`-4 / -8`**.
</objective>

## The motion control's new home

`Help & shortcuts` is the footer link the PDF draws on pages 2, 3, 4 and 5 (`Help & shortcuts · Device
actions`, bottom right). The control that lands there is:

- a plain checkbox or two-state button, **not** a segmented four-option switch - there are no longer
  four layers to switch;
- it **stops the ambient previews** (the shared rAF host's `visible` predicate) and leaves the
  interactive preview alone, which is §6's *"animate only the selected or explicitly previewed card"*
  read as a user control;
- it is **additive to `prefers-reduced-motion`, never subtractive**: a user whose OS asks for reduced
  motion cannot be given motion by this control. State that as a rule and gate it;
- its choice persists through the same guarded `localStorage` shape `ScreenToggle.svelte:81` used -
  **copy the guard, not the component** - and 13-06 will fold that key into the store module.

Two strings: the control's label and its one-line explanation. Both in D-05's register, both ledgered.
