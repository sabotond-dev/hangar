<objective>
The gallery, and the one routing decision this phase is not allowed to make by itself.

**What already works and is not being rebuilt.** `src/routes/browse/+page.svelte` is 659 lines of
working machinery: the wall of sixteen live pads behind one clock, one tab stop across the grid,
`IntersectionObserver` gating, the sort radiogroup, the search landmark, the live region, the
`sessionStorage` browse-return store that restores scroll, filters and search. §6 asks for *"return to
the previous scroll position, filters, and search after closing a configuration"* and
`readBrowseReturn` **already is that**. **The logic keeps; the chrome is rewritten.**

**What changes, and each has a decision behind it.**

- **The rail's `MADE FOR` rows are derived, not typed.** The PDF shows four (`Modulation`,
  `Notes & chords`, `Visuals`, `Expression`); the spec proposes three; HANGAR has a closed vocabulary
  with no singletons and asserted histogram floors. D-11 chose *"HANGAR's eight FOR terms… honest to
  the catalog"* - and **Phase 12's 12-04 retires `keys`, so the honest number is seven.** The decision's
  own reason is satisfied only by deriving the list from `FOR_TERMS` at runtime and asserting the count
  as observed. 13-VALIDATION records this as D-4; the SUMMARY records D-11's "eight" as superseded by
  12-04, by name and dated.
- **The FEELS filter row is dropped** (D-11, and the PDF has no Character filter). The six FEELS terms
  survive **as card metadata** in the `MODULATION · FLOWING` line, which is where the PDF puts them.
  `FacetRow` keeps its radiogroup mechanics, its arrow-key selection and its 44px targets, and renders
  **once**.
- **Sort becomes a `<select>`** (PDF). `CAT-02` is unchanged in substance - Featured and Name, no
  popularity metrics, nothing faked - and takes a qualifier at 13-20 rather than an amendment.
- **The card** becomes the PDF's: a square preview, the name **plus a favorite star**, **one** category
  and **one** tag where today there are three tags, one sentence, and a full-width `Explore` action.
  D-14 Q11c settles which sentence: `description` on the card, `quiet` as helper text in the workspace.
  `listing.spec.ts` still requires a `quiet` line for every entry whose motion is not self-evident, and
  that requirement does not move.
- **The card is still the link and the button is inside it.** `BrowseLink.svelte` already navigates
  around this problem and the whole-card link is stronger for keyboard users; the `Explore` button is
  the visible affordance. Say which element carries the accessible name so a screen reader hears one
  link and not two.

**The decision this plan cannot make.** The spec routes a configuration at `/playground/:id`; the PDF's
breadcrumb says `PLAYGROUND / ARC`; and HANGAR's share stamp, its OG images, its `SITE_ORIGIN` joins,
three landing states and a 516-line codec with a 592-line spec and a 469-line sweep all live at
`/c/<id>`. **The site has never been public** - `SHARE-03`'s own amendment says so, which is why this is
cheap now and expensive later - but it moves the unfurl target and a thousand lines of tests, so it is
the user's. It is asked **first**, before a card links anywhere.

Purpose: the screen most visitors will spend their time on, built on the machinery that already works.
Output: one new route, one old one retired or forwarded per the answer, four components re-skinned,
`browse-ui.spec.ts` 6 -> 9. Term `+0 / +3`.
</objective>

**The `MADE FOR` rows carry no counts** (the PDF shows none) and their number is whatever `FOR_TERMS`
holds. Assert the observed count and print it; do not assert seven, do not assert eight.

## The chips, one row

`Use` then `All`, then one chip per **category**. The PDF's four chips are a subset of its own rail;
HANGAR's chips are the same list as the rail or a subset of it, and **the two must not disagree** -
derive both from `FOR_TERMS` and let the chip row show the same members. If the row is too wide at
seven, **it wraps** rather than being truncated to four, because a truncated facet row hides
configurations from the visitor and §6 requires the actual matching count and a way to clear each
filter.
