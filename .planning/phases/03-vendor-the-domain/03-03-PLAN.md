The trap this plan is shaped around: `cost().used` is `Math.max(measure(lua), lua.length) + reserved`

every case). A grid-protocol bump whose minifier spent five more characters would move
`compressScript(...).length` and NOT move `cost().used` - the bump gate would stay green through
precisely the change it exists to catch. So the fixture records four numbers per preset and the pin
gate asserts the compressed pair.
