### The T3 surface, and nothing else

The complete list of names a hand-authored entry may call, from 09-01's `HOST_GLOBALS` and
`HOST_SELF_METHODS`. `src/lib/catalog/host-surface.spec.ts` refuses everything outside it.

```

        glt(a,l,t)    glpfs(a,l,p,f,s)  glim(v,lo,hi)  gtt(slot,ms)
```

**`gln`, `gld` and `glx` do not exist here.** Every published ZONA recipe uses them; `glc` is the only
colour call HANGAR has. `glc(a, l, r, g, b, 1)` - with the sixth argument forcing the minimum stop
black - is the idiom for every card. A bare `gms(...)` raises; `gmms`, `gmbs` and `gks` are bare.

### The seven traps, and which of these three contains which

1. **`glag` is not the identity.** Even rows are mirrored in hardware; the lookup does the correction.
   Always `glag(0, x + y*9)`, never a computed hardware index.
2. **F2Ieq.** A fractional argument to a firmware call becomes **0**, silently. Every division is
   `//`; every `math.sqrt` or `math.atan` result is closed with `// 1`.
3. **Colour channels truncate, never clamp.** 260 renders as 4. Every colour knob value keeps all
   three channels inside 0..255 by construction, and the header says so.
4. **Event codes.** Onset is `e == 4 or e > 8`; an end is `e >= 5`. Code 9 is a tap arriving as one
   message with no separate lift. `e == 5` alone leaks a stuck note and the vendored scanner raises
   `trap-end-of-contact` on it.
5. **`glp(n, l, -1)` does nothing on ZONA.** Always pass an explicit 0..255 phase.
6. **The keeper pitfall.** `glt(a, L, 65535)` on a layer carrying a decaying trail replaces the
   countdown, the rate wraps past zero and the cell strobes forever.
   `lua-smoke.spec.ts` test 3 fails on "timeout at least 64511 together with decay rate at least 200".
7. **Token prefixes.** `renderLua` substitutes by plain `String.replaceAll`, so no token may be a
   prefix of another in the same entry - `@TRAIL` inside `@TRAILC` renders the colour as `42C`.
   `catalog.spec.ts:178-187` gates it.

### One layer is half a colour

`grid_led_render` divides the accumulated stops by 512 after summing all three layers, and one layer
can never exceed **254/512 = 49.6 %** of the value you ask for. A card that must look bright paints
the same value on layers 1 **and** 2. Layer 0 is the alert layer and is never written.

Note that `planLayers`'s mutual exclusions are a **compiler** rule and do not bind a hand-authored
entry - a `lua` entry owns layers 1 and 2 directly. What does bind is the arithmetic above and the
fact that there are two free layers, not three. Each entry below states which layer carries what.

### Let firmware animate

`grid_led_tick` advances every armed layer's phase in C every ~10 ms with no Lua running. Arm once in
Setup with `glpfs` plus `glt` and the grid animates itself for up to 655 s at zero cost. Motion comes
from staggering the **starting phase** per cell, not from a Lua loop. Rate is uint8 and wraps: `-1`
becomes 255 and runs the phase backwards. On the last tick firmware sets the rate to 0, so the
animation freezes and `glt` alone will not restart it - re-issue `glf` or `glpfs`.

**The card.** A latching effect pad. Slide anywhere to set an X and a Y; lift, and the value stays
where you left it, with the cell you lifted from lit and breathing. Touch again and it moves.

**Route: `lua`.** `sends.toggle` is read only when `sends.kind` is `"zones"` (`_pad.ts`, the `sends`
sheet), and `spring` only when it is `"xy"`. A latching XY is in neither branch, so it is not
expressible as a `PadState` at all. Write that sentence in the header.

**Not ARC.** ARC is an LFO you shape and leave running - *"the swirl shows the rate"*, and the
animation IS the data. HOLD holds a **static** value and shows you where it is. The header must state
the distinction and the description must not read like ARC's.

**Mechanism.**

- Setup paints a dim frame - the 32 border cells - on layer 2 in `@RINGC`, static, so the card is
  legible with no finger on it and the OG image is not black. `restsBlack: false`.
- Setup also lights a default held cell at the pad's centre, so the latch is visible on arrival and
  the recorded frames at ticks 0, 37, 101, 500 and 1009 show a breathing cell.
- `self.h` holds the currently held cell's index. On every sample with `e == 4 or e > 8 or e == 1`
  (onset or move), compute `n = x*9//128 + y*9//128*9`; if `n ~= self.h`, darken the old cell with
  `glc(a,1,0,0,0,1)` and `glc(a,2,0,0,0,1)` - **one pass, never erase-then-paint the whole grid** -
  then paint the new one on **both** layers in `@COL` and arm it with
  `glpfs(a,L,0,@RATE,3)` and `glt(a,L,60000)`. Shape 3 is the sine, which is what makes it breathe
  rather than flash. Painting both layers is what makes it bright: one layer caps at 49.6 %.
- On every sample, send `self:gms(@CH, 176, @CC, x*127//128, 0)` and
  `self:gms(@CH, 176, @CC + 1, y*127//128, 0)`. Two 7-bit CCs. On a lift (`e >= 5`) send nothing and
  reset nothing - that is the whole feature.
- **The 655 s ceiling.** `glt(a, L, 60000)` is 600 s and the Timer re-arms it. The Timer's whole job
  is one line: re-arm itself with `gtt(0, @PERIOD)` and re-issue `glpfs` on the held cell. Nothing
  else. Without it the breathing freezes after ten minutes on a visitor's real hardware, and a card
  that dies after ten minutes is a broken card.

**Traps this entry contains, for the header.** F2Ieq on the two `x*9//128` divisions (both floored).
The colour knob values keep every channel inside 0..255. The onset filter is `e == 4 or e > 8`, and
`e == 5` appears nowhere. `glp` is never called with a negative phase. The keeper is 60000, three
orders of magnitude below 65535, on a layer whose rate is single-digit - which is exactly the shape
`lua-smoke.spec.ts` test 3's floor discriminates as legitimate, and the header must say **do not
"fix" it to 65535**.

**The honest limit, for the card copy.** Two things, and both belong in the header:

1. Single contact by design. A second finger moves the same value; there is no per-contact latch and
   the character budget would not carry one.

   permanently stuck contact - and `pad-sim.ts:262-268` states plainly that the simulator *"cannot
   manufacture the stuck contact"*. **A latching configuration can look perfect in a browser and stick
   on hardware.** This is D-11, it is the reason HOLD gets a bench row in task 3, and the sentence

### STEPS

**The card.** An eight by eight step grid. Tap a cell to arm it; a bright column sweeps left to right
and plays what you armed on the way past.

**Route: `lua`.** There is no sequencer anywhere in the `PadState` vocabulary - `sends.kind` is
`none | xy | zones | faders | trackpad | dial` and none of them has a clock. Say so in the header.

**Not EUCLID, not SONAR.** EUCLID is three concentric rings beating against each other on a 48-tick
cycle; SONAR is a radial sweep through 16 angle buckets. STEPS is the plain rectangular grid both of
those deliberately are not - eight tracks, eight steps, a column you can read at a glance. Phase 8
dropped a nine-step sequencer for overlapping EUCLID's slot and kept it as a follow-on
(`08-06-SUMMARY.md:628-629`); this is that follow-on at the size the pad actually is. State the
distinction in the header.

**Mechanism.**

- The armed grid is `x` in 0..7 by `y` in 0..7. The ninth column and the ninth row are dark by design
  and the header says so - eight by eight is what a step sequencer is, and inventing a ninth track to
  fill the pad would be filling the pad rather than making an instrument.
- `self.p` is a flat 64-entry table of booleans. Setup arms a default pattern - the bottom row on
  every second step, which reads as four-on-the-floor - so **the card is lit and playing before
  anyone touches it**. That is what makes it a card rather than a black square, and it is what puts a
  real frame in `frames.json` and in the OG image. `restsBlack: false`.
- Armed cells are painted dim on layer 2 in `@ARMC`, static. The sweeping column is painted on layer 1
  in `@SWEEPC` with `glpfs(a,1,255,250,0)` and `glt(a,1,@TRAIL)` - phase 255, a large rate so the
  ramp runs down fast, and a short timeout - which is the shipped self-erasing trail idiom: min and
  mid black, only max coloured, so the cell fades to exact black in C with no erase pass.
- The Timer re-arms itself **first** (`gtt(0,@TEMPO)`), matching the compiler's own gtt-first bodies,
  then advances `self.k`, then paints the column and plays it: for each row `r` in 0..7 where
  `self.p[k + r*8]` is true, `self:gms(@CH, 144, @NOTE + r, 100, 0)` and a matching note-off for the
  previous column's row. Note-off before note-on in the same body, so a held note never overlaps
  itself.
- Touch: onset only (`e == 4 or e > 8`). Compute the cell, ignore anything with `x*9//128 > 7` or
  `y*9//128 > 7`, toggle `self.p`, repaint that one cell.

**Traps for the header.** The `//` on both coordinate divisions. Eight-row note range means
`@NOTE + 7` must stay under 128 for every `@NOTE` value - state the arithmetic. The trail is a decay,
so **no keeper is written on layer 1 at all**. Colour channels inside 0..255.

**The honest limit, for the card copy.** Eight rows is eight voices, and the pad has no way to show
you what the host is playing - there is no inbound MIDI in this phase (D-04), so the column tells you
where the sequencer is, not where your DAW is.

### SLAM

**The card.** Nine drum zones. How high in the pad you hit is how hard it plays, and the height of
the bloom is the velocity you sent.

**Route: `lua`.** `sends.velocity` is a literal 1..127 in `PadState` and `_pad.ts:293` records that
*"From position" has no measured recipe* - velocity from position is not in the vocabulary, and that
is the whole card. Say so in the header.

**Not ninepads, not CHORUS.** `ninepads` lights the zone you are holding at a fixed velocity; CHORUS
fires a whole triad and blooms outward. SLAM's bloom is not decoration - **the number of lit rows is
the velocity byte**, which is what turns the module's missing pressure sensor into something you can
see. Say that in the header, because it is the argument for the card.

**Mechanism.**

- Nine 3x3 zones. Setup paints a static outline - the four corner cells of each zone - on layer 2 in
  `@ZONEC`, so the nine zones are countable at rest. `restsBlack: false`.
- On onset (`e == 4 or e > 8`): zone `z = x*3//128 + y*3//128*3`. Velocity from height:
  `v = (127 - y)*126//127 + 1`, so the top of the pad is 127 and the bottom is 1. All floored.
- The bloom: light `n = v*3//127 + 1` rows of that zone from the bottom up, on both layers in `@COL`,
  with `glpfs` phase 255 and a decay rate so `glt(a,L,@DECAY)` fades them to black in C. One pass.
- `self:gms(@CH, 144, @NOTE + z, v, 0)` for the note-on.
- **Code 9.** A tap arrives as one message with no separate lift, so the handler must send the
  note-off itself: `if e == 9 then self:gms(@CH, 128, @NOTE + z, 0, 0) end` immediately after the
  note-on. On `e >= 5` send the note-off for the zone stored in `self.z[i]`, keyed by contact id, and
  clear it. Keying per-contact state by `id` is free (five records of four fields is about 850 bytes)
  and is what stops two fingers cancelling each other.
- No Timer is needed. **Give it one anyway**, one line: `gtt(0,@DECAY_PERIOD)` is not required, but a
  Timer that re-arms nothing is a wasted event. Ship SLAM with `timer: ""` - the same shape MORPH
  uses (`entries/morph.ts:65`) - and state in the header that the zone outline is static and needs no
  re-arming, so there is nothing for a Timer to do. `createLuaPadSim` maps `""` onto `undefined` for
  the host; `docs/HARDWARE-AUDITION.md` row 1's install-order rule already carries MORPH's exemption
  and this entry joins it.

**Traps for the header.** All four divisions floored. `@NOTE + 8` under 128 for every `@NOTE` value.
Code 9 handled explicitly, and `e == 5` nowhere. Per-contact state keyed by `id`. Colour channels
inside 0..255. The decay is a decay - no keeper anywhere.

**The honest limit, for the card copy.** Two fingers in one zone: the highlight clears when the first
one leaves, which is the documented caveat on every zone-based configuration
(`ZONA_GUI_SPEC.md` section 4.2). And height is not force - the card is honest about measuring where
you hit, not how hard.

| `zone` | Outline colour | `colour` | `@ZONEC` | four dim triples | index 0 |
| `decay` | Bloom length | `feel` | `@DECAY` | `"12"`, `"24"`, `"42"`, `"64"` | index 1 |
