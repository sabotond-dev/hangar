none at all.

### The two floors, and why they are raised rather than derived

`src/lib/og/build.spec.ts:215` and `e2e/artifacts.e2e.ts:69` both assert `ROUTED.length >= 16`. They
are **non-vacuity floors**, not counts: they exist so a spec that walks an empty set cannot pass. A
floor of 16 against a catalog of 36 has stopped saying anything, so both rise to **36**. They stay
literals rather than becoming `ROUTED.length`, because `expect(ROUTED.length).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(ROUTED.length)`
is a tautology - the whole point is a number a human chose. Say that in a comment beside each.

### The ceiling comment

`src/lib/ui/BrowseGrid.svelte:65-68` says: *"past roughly 40 entries, revisit with lazy mounting or
virtualisation - the limit there is compositor memory for mounted canvases, not simulation."* It was
written at sixteen. At thirty-six it is four entries from its own line and it needs a measured note
rather than a projection - not a code change, a comment that records what thirty-six actually cost.
