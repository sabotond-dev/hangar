**`math.random` is forbidden** by `lua-entries.sweep.spec.ts` test 4, and so are `string.`, `os.`,
`io.`, the module loaders, the metatable API, `goto` and the label syntax. Arithmetic pseudo-noise
keyed on a counter is the shipped alternative and is what the vendored `shimmer` look does.

`gln`/`gld`/`glx` do not exist; `glc(a,l,r,g,b,1)` is the only colour call.

### The protocol ceiling these three can actually hit

`GRID_LUA_STDO_LENGTH` is **256 bytes**, cleared once per 10 ms cycle. A `gms` voice message is 14
bytes, so about **eighteen MIDI messages fit in one cycle** and an append that does not fit is
**refused with no error**. LIFE can produce dozens of births in one generation and SNAKE can eat
often; both must rate-limit their own sends. Write the ceiling and the limit into each header.

`coroutine` does not exist, so any "spread this over several cycles" design is a hand-rolled state
machine on `self.<field>`. **Bound every loop**: an infinite loop in `touch_cb` hangs the port task
forever with the watchdog's panic disabled, and recovery is a power cycle.

### The seven traps

`glag(0, x + y*9)` always. Every division `//`; every `math.*` result closed with `// 1`. Colour
channels truncate rather than clamp. Onset is `e == 4 or e > 8`, an end is `e >= 5`, code 9 is a tap
with no separate lift. Never `glp(n,l,-1)`. Never a 65535 keeper on a decaying layer. No token may be
a prefix of another.

One layer caps at 49.6 % of the colour asked for; paint both for brightness. Layer 0 is never written.

### The copy rules (09-02's gate enforces every one)

One sentence, second person, present tense, active voice, naming the payoff. At most 110 characters,
one line, distinct. No exclamation mark, no emoji, no ASCII apostrophe between letters, no hyphen as a
dash, no ellipsis in a description. Four feel tags, each in `KNOWN_TAGS` or added on purpose. A single
upper-case word for the name. No **aurora**, no **ghost** - and note that the reserved word applies to
**every field**, so a description reading "a ghost of the last shape" is refused. The gate will say so.

### The knob rule

No sixteen-value channel knob. At most four: `"0"`, `"1"`, `"9"`, `"15"`.

### ETCH

**The card.** Draw on the pad with a finger and it stays. Sweep across it fast and the whole thing
wipes clean.

**Route: `lua`.** An eighty-one cell persistent canvas. `touch.kind` offers
`none | comet | perFinger | bloom | glow | disturb`, every one of them a self-erasing decay - a
persistent mark is the one thing the touch sheet is built not to do.

**Mechanism.**

- `self.c` is an eighty-one entry table of 0 or 1. On any live sample, set the cell under the finger
  and paint it in `@INKC` on both layers with **no timeout at all** - a cell with `glt` unset simply
  stays at the phase it was given, which is exactly a persistent mark and costs nothing.
- **The wipe.** Store the previous sample's cell index per contact id. If the Chebyshev distance
  between consecutive cells exceeds `@WIPE`, clear `self.c` and black all eighty-one cells in one
  pass. That is one full repaint on a gesture a visitor makes deliberately, so the touch budget is
  not at risk - but say in the header that it is the one place this entry repaints everything, and
  why it is affordable.
- Each newly drawn cell sends `self:gms(@CH, 144, @NOTE + (8 - row), 90, 0)` and an immediate note-off:
  the row is the pitch, so drawing plays a line. **Rate-limit to one note per sample** - one sample is
  one cycle, so this is inside the eighteen-message ceiling by construction, and the header says so.
- `timer: ""`. Nothing evolves; a drawing stays until you wipe it.
- **`restsBlack: true`.** A blank canvas is black, and that is the configuration rather than a broken
  picture. That means `motion: "dark"` and the `quiet` line must be `RESTS_DARK_NOTE` **verbatim** -
  `listing.spec.ts:243-250` asserts it byte for byte. It also means the OG image is a black square,
  which `gen-og.mjs`'s gate permits precisely because the entry declared it. The catalog needs its
  dark cards - `listing.spec.ts:204-208` requires all three motions to occur - and this is one.

**Traps for the header.** No timeout on the ink layer, marked **do-not-add**: a `glt` on a persistent
mark would make it fade, and a 65535 keeper on it would be the pitfall-1 shape. Every division
floored. Colour channels inside 0..255. Per-contact previous-cell storage keyed by `id`, cleared on
an end event so a lift does not leave a stale coordinate that triggers a spurious wipe on the next
touch.

**The honest limit, for the card copy.** A wipe is a gesture, so a genuinely fast stroke will erase
what you meant to draw - which is why the threshold is a knob.

### LIFE

**The card.** Conway's Game of Life on the pad. Tap to seed it, and the pattern plays itself out.

**Route: `lua`.** A per-cell automaton with a double buffer. Nothing in `PadState` evolves state.

**Mechanism.**

- `self.g` is the current generation, eighty-one entries; `self.h` is the next. **Compute into `h`,
  then swap** - never in place, or the neighbour counts are wrong for half the grid. Say that in the
  header; it is the single most likely bug in this entry.
- The grid is a **torus**: neighbours wrap in both axes, `(x + dx) % 9` and `(y + dy) % 9`, both
  floored. A torus is free and it lets a glider survive, which is the difference between a card that
  is alive and one that settles in four generations.
- Setup seeds a **glider** at a fixed position, so the card evolves from tick 0 and the golden frames
  at 0, 37, 101, 500 and 1009 are five different real generations. `restsBlack: false`.
- The Timer re-arms first with `gtt(0, @GEN)`, computes the generation, and paints: live cells in
  `@LIVEC` on both layers; cells that **just died** in `@DYINGC` on layer 2 with a short decay so the
  pattern leaves a trace; everything else black. One pass over eighty-one cells per generation, which
  at the slowest `@GEN` is well inside the Timer's budget - but the loop is bounded by a literal and
  the header says why.
- Sound: at most `@VOICES` notes per generation, one per birth in scan order, then stop counting.
  **This is the protocol ceiling made explicit** - eighteen messages per 10 ms cycle, and a busy
  generation on a torus can have twenty births. Write the ceiling, the limit and the consequence of
  exceeding it (an append that does not fit is refused with no error) into the header.
- After a settle - if the generation is identical to the previous one, or to the one before that -
  re-seed the glider. Two stored hashes, a couple of characters each. Without it the card dies to a
  still life within a minute and a browse page full of dead grids is not a card. Mark it
  do-not-remove.

**Traps for the header.** The double buffer, marked do-not-simplify. Every division and modulo
floored. The bounded loops. The note ceiling. Colour channels inside 0..255. Code 9: a tap seeds a
cell and must work as a fast tap.

**The honest limit, for the card copy.** Eighty-one cells on a torus is a very small universe - most
seeds settle in seconds, which is why tapping matters and why the card re-seeds itself.

| `live` | Living colour | `colour` | `@LIVEC` | four bright triples | index 0 |
