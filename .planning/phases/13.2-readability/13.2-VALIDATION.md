## Why the phase is serial, and the order it runs in

**01 -> 02 -> 03 -> 04 -> 05 -> 06.** One tree, one `build/`, one port 4173 with
`reuseExistingServer: true`, one `test-results/`, `artifacts.e2e.ts` reading `HEAD`, one
`gate/` directory whose `--after` must be compared to the `--before` of the same plan, 0.34 GB free
for one `--maxWorkers=2` run — and the substantive chain the research states (C.2): 03 before 04
(the store's header cites the copy modules' retirement lines), 03 before 05 (`TuningRegion.svelte`
cites `tune/copy.ts`), 01 before everything (the gate script and the shared helper), 06 last (the
gate). The `wave` field is that order; every `depends_on` is the immediate predecessor.
