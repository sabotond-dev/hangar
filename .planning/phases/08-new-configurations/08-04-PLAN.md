**The `@` trap.** The event marker is `--[[@cb]]` and it contains an `@`. A leftover-token check of
the form "no `@` remains after substitution" is therefore **permanently red**. Check for
`@` followed by an upper-case letter instead - the token grammar is `^@[A-Z][A-Z0-9_]*$`, and `@cb`
is lower case, so the two cannot collide.

   - every knob's `token` occurs at least once across the two templates;
   - **no knob value contains an `@` followed by an upper-case letter.** A value that looked like a
